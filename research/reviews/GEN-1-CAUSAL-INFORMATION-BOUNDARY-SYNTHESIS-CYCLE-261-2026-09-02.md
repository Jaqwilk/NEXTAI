## Minimal causal information boundary

For any candidate output, target-useful information can enter through only the
following candidate-visible parents under the present scientific boundary:

1. source observations or learned source state;
2. target-local support observations or revealed outcomes;
3. chronological interaction with the target environment;
4. an external label, reward, verifier, constraint or success signal;
5. fixed code, interface, architectural, evolutionary or hardware prior;
6. external/environmental state that stores earlier information.

Global fitting, local updating, search, relaxation, routing, compilation,
caching and indexing are transformations or storage operations. They can
extract, combine, discard, retain or amortize information, but they are not new
information-bearing edges. Moving state into the environment similarly changes
the carrier and accounting boundary, not its origin.

`research/checks/causal_information_boundary_synthesis_cycle_261.json`.

## What the apparent gains actually used

The portfolio repeatedly produced real but narrower signatures: bounded local
updates, near-zero knowledge-size query slope, short critical paths, reduced
search nodes, smaller residuals and warm reuse. None constitutes the required
causal gain by itself.

- When target-local observations were enough, posterior means, PPM/CTW,
  persistence, RLS/Kalman, exact algorithms or native adaptive solvers supplied
  the useful statistic with less total work.
- When foreign data or pooled meta-fit was added, the matched independent or
  support-only learner recovered the useful part, or a family collapsed. The
  cycle-256 audit found zero L1 survivors among all 14 cross-family-capable
  hypotheses.
- When exactness was preserved, the complete solver, verifier or constraint
  already carried correctness; learned search guidance did not repay its
  acquisition and fit cost.

Thus the learned transformation often changed a representation or surrogate,
but did not introduce target-useful information unavailable to its strongest
matched control.
