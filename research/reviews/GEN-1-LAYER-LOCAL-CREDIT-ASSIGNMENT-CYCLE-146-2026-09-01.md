## OBSERVATION

The 63-result portfolio has tested many representations and inference
substrates: exact and learned programs, sparse indices, recurrent state,
predictive quotients, cellular rules, energy relaxation, probabilistic
circuits, active acquisition, associative memory, masked refinement,
compression, cross-family transfer, online adaptation, operator compilation and
event-sparse local state. In almost all trainable candidates, however, credit
was assigned by a global solve, backpropagation-like optimization, meta-fit,
enumeration or a supplied local target. “Local update” usually meant that only
one test slot changed, not that hidden layers learned without a reverse credit
path. Layer-local credit assignment is therefore a real portfolio gap.

EXP-0004 also supplies a warning: reducing update work from `17,066.67/21,162.67`
to `90` was not enough because worst-file quality regressed by `0.24858` against
the best complete control. A new local-learning mechanism must be compared at
matched quality and full cost, not rewarded for locality alone.

The primary literature establishes three nearby mechanisms. Forward-Forward
(SRC-0187) uses positive and negative forward passes with a separate goodness
objective at every layer. Equilibrium propagation (SRC-0188) uses free and
target-nudged relaxation of one energy circuit. Feedback alignment (SRC-0189)
removes exact transpose transport but still sends a downstream error through a
reverse pathway. None is novel to NEXTAI, and none of the cited work establishes
a full-system advantage over universal sequence models.

## CANDIDATE MECHANISMS

### A. Forward-Forward layer-local goodness — select

This is the cleanest one-factor change. A source-identical neural architecture
can be trained either with independent layer goodness losses or with ordinary
end-to-end credit, while initialization, data order, update count, output rule,
query path and state remain fixed. A frozen-hidden version separates useful
hidden learning from output-layer fitting. The main role is auditable: an
earlier layer may read only its input, its own activations, its own weights and
the positive/negative flag.

### B. Equilibrium propagation — reject for the first scout

Free/nudged phases introduce recurrent energy dynamics, a relaxation tolerance,
phase length and symmetric weights. The portfolio already tested energy
relaxation, and a result would mix credit assignment with a new inference
substrate and adaptive depth. This has lower information per engineering cost.

### C. Feedback alignment — retain only as later context

Feedback alignment is source-identical and simple, but it still communicates a
global downstream error through fixed feedback weights. It changes weight
transport, not the stronger question of whether useful hidden credit can be
assigned without a backward path. Testing it first would be a smaller causal
departure.

## COHORT SELECTION

The active WT cohort is unsuitable for Forward-Forward because continuous
trajectory generation has no unique mechanical negative-label construction;
adding a decoder or continuous search would confound credit assignment with a
new output model. Finite predictive-state and local-cellular cohorts provide
exhaustive tiny transition tables that CSSR/FSM controls already solve, so a
positive neural result would only relearn a supplied finite quotient.

The existing whole-file repository-compression corpus is the best discriminator.
Next-byte prediction provides an unambiguous positive label and mechanically
generated wrong labels. It uses real held-out files, a strict predict-then-reveal
boundary, and strong uniform, unigram, true PPM, true CTW, LZ and dense
autoregressive controls. Mean, cold and worst-file bits per byte prevent online
adaptation from hiding failed transfer.

Historical `heldout_repository_sequence_compression_v1` cannot be reused as if
unchanged: it registered only two K values, predates the current universal
Pareto/final-schema gates and lacks source-identical credit-assignment
ablations. A prospective v2 must preserve the corpus, split, target, metric
formulas, state boundary and every classical algorithm while changing only the
matrix and prospective neural roles listed in
`research/checks/layer_local_credit_assignment_selection_v1.json`.

## INTERPRETATION

The selected question is not whether a neural byte model can beat CTW after
architecture search. It is whether a known layer-local learning law preserves
the capability of an otherwise identical globally trained network while
reducing fully charged fit work or peak training state. The universal coding
controls then decide whether either neural route has system-level value.

This isolates a fundamental mechanism missing from the portfolio and is
falsifiable in one cheap run. It deliberately expects a strong classical null:
Forward-Forward may require expensive negative construction or lose predictive
information, while CTW may remain better and cheaper. Such a valid negative is
useful and ends the exact rule without width, threshold, learning-rate or
negative-sampling search.

## CONFIDENCE AND LIMITATIONS

Confidence is `0.93` that layer-local credit assignment is an untested causal
