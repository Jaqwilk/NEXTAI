The repeated scientific signal is not a shortage of small implementation fixes.
Anonymous columns do not provide a common semantic coordinate system, and every

## Three candidate principles

### 1. Learned latent coordinate canonicalization — reject without scoring

A permutation-equivariant encoder could try to align anonymous channels through
lagged predictive signatures. This is not independent of the failed portfolio:
it recreates spectral sharing and predictive-equivalence indexing, with the same
identifiability problem and extra fit/state cost. Any post-result choice of
signature, rank or neighborhood would be tuning a dormant family.

### 2. Observation-learned source affinity and sparse routing — reject without scoring

A gate could select source models using public-support likelihood. This is a hard
or soft expert mixture plus an index. Earlier modular routing, active acquisition,
predictive indexing and the convex algorithmic prior already show the relevant
failure mode: classical lookup/tree/mixture controls match the capability with
less full cost, while acquiring and maintaining the gate scales with stored
knowledge. Family labels would make the proposal still less admissible.

### 3. Transfer the local update law, not world parameters — select provisionally

The only surviving independent contrast is whether experience can teach one
family-blind update algorithm that adapts a freshly initialized, world-local
predictor better than fixed LMS/RLS/Kalman updates. No fitted world parameter,
latent coordinate, residual trajectory, index or expert weight is shared. Only
the update rule is meta-learned from anonymous source trajectories; test-world
state starts identically and changes through local public observations.

This does not revive the exact `shared_meta_update` from EXP-0043. That
implementation remains discarded and its learning-rate, gate, features and bank
must not be tuned. EXP-0043 nevertheless left a specific generality ambiguity:
every implementable method had zero worst-phase accuracy under short randomly
switched phases, while the privileged segmented control was exact. The frozen
three-family continuous cohort instead has identifiable public support and strong
ridge/RLS/empirical controls, so it can test update-rule transfer without changing
the benchmark. Prior art in Meta-AF (SRC-0163) means a positive would be evidence
for transferable capability/cost under this contract, not novelty of learned
optimizers.

Selection confidence is low (`0.18`). This is the highest-information surviving
test, not a promising result. It must be killed after one valid negative quick;
no optimizer-depth, feature, learning-rate or state-size tuning is authorized.

## Frozen requirements for the later discriminator

The cheapest admissible test uses the existing
`heldout_three_family_continuous_transfer_v6` cohort unchanged at K=`4/6/9`.
Before implementation or scoring, a later immutable plan must freeze:

- one source-identical update-rule implementation and constants for shared,
  independent, cross-family-only and support-only roles; roles may differ only
  in allowed source data;
- identical fresh world-local predictor initialization for every role and no
  transfer of fitted predictor weights or hidden world state;
- no family names, paths, native types, semantic channel names, hand ontology or
  evaluator-provided routing;
- persistence, tensor ridge ARX, tensor RLS ARX, empirical Gaussian joint,
  contextual Gaussian Chow–Liu and autoregressive controls, plus the four causal
  ablations, under the existing full acquisition/fit/query/update/state/R16 cost;
- a development-only minimum meaningful effect above noise before scoring;
- success only if shared beats independent and cross-family-only beats
  support-only in every family, worst-family quality does not regress, all
  stability gates pass, and the learner is not Pareto-dominated by a fixed
  classical updater;
- the qualitative signature of bounded world-local update cost at all three K
  scales. A cost slope alone cannot rescue a quality failure, and one positive
  seed may authorize only an unchanged three-seed replication.
