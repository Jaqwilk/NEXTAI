# GEN-1 closed-loop environmental computation search — cycle 251

## Scope and integrity

This is one bounded no-scoring SEARCH MODE cycle. It asks whether a learner can
modify or exploit its ordinary environment so that later exact computation is
cheaper, rather than storing an internal representation or selecting a route.
It changes no hypothesis, plan, candidate, benchmark, evaluator, runner,
schema, manifest, seed or score. The persistent user objective authorizes
continued SEARCH MODE instead of a causal alias forced by cadence.

## Complete causal partition

Every inspected proposal falls into one of three cases.

### 1. Explicit environmental writes and reads

Digital pheromones, marks, files, tool-side graphs and shared blackboards are
external memory and communication. ForMIC (`SRC-0267`) learns useful policies,
but the environment already supplies pheromone deposition, local sensing,
decay and spatial semantics; stable training additionally uses a pheromone
curriculum and action filtering. A source-identical null can write the same
bytes to a hash table, event log, dependency trace or classical pheromone map.

NEXTAI has directly covered this case. Exact caches, dependency traces,
predictive indexes, sparse memories and operator libraries already show that
warm work can fall after an explicit write. The unresolved cost is acquiring
the key and maintaining the state, not whether storage can be placed outside
the candidate process.

### 2. Passive environmental dynamics

Physical traces can genuinely compute. `SRC-0268` shows self-propelled
particles opening transient paths in passive colloids that feed back on later
motion without signalling or processing. This is a real counterexample to the
claim that useful memory must be internal. It is not an observation-learned
rule: the substrate dynamics and task consequence are fixed physical facts.

Origami (`SRC-0269`), chemical (`SRC-0270`) and mycelium (`SRC-0271`)
reservoirs similarly exploit body or material dynamics. Their common causal
form is a fixed nonlinear reservoir plus designed input/sensing and a trained
linear or ridge readout. Manufacturing, actuation, equilibration, chemistry,
mass spectrometry, carrier boards and analog conditioning are part of the
system boundary. The different substrates do not share one executable learner
or interface, and their energy/latency observations are hardware evidence, not
an algorithmic cross-family scaling law.

### 3. Transforming the problem instance

Canonicalization, compiling, sorting, indexing, precomputing kernels, building
a hierarchy and arranging a workspace all move work before later queries. This
is preprocessing, program synthesis, data-structure construction or
preconditioning. It is useful only if acquisition plus transformation
amortizes over a preregistered horizon.

The local history is decisive:

- `EXP-20260830-0023` obtained a narrow dependency-trace warm/update benefit
  but relied on manual canonicalization and more state;
- `EXP-20260901-0030` found learned active acquisition indistinguishable from
  cheaper Gaussian/kernel controls after full cost;
- `EXP-20260901-0034` made depth-independent queries by exact dyadic operator
  precomposition, but fit/update/state made R16 more than four times costlier
  than matched ridge;
- `EXP-20260901-0036` reduced exact search nodes through a learned order, but
  its training cost lost to the frozen exact order at R1/R4/R16;
- `EXP-20260902-0001` built a learned hierarchy yet cost about `11.5x` the R16
  work of target standard/adaptive SA without foreign-source causal gain.

Changing the storage location from internal state to an evaluator-owned world
does not change any of these causal identities or remove their costs.

## Admission test

An admissible fourth case would require all of the following:

1. the write/read or physical interaction channel arises naturally and is not
   a hand-designed semantic memory API;
2. the transformation is learned from observations and has an independently
   checkable consequence;
3. one source-identical rule and constants operate across at least three
   qualitatively different environments;
4. explicit-memory, active-sensing, indexing, caching, compiling,
   preconditioning and fixed-reservoir controls cannot supply the effect;
5. acquisition, writes, reads, sensing, substrate dynamics, manufacture or
   simulation, updates, state and reuse horizon are fully charged.

No inspected primary result or existing NEXTAI cohort meets even conditions
1–3 jointly. A digital benchmark must define writable cells or tools and thus
creates case 1 or 3. A software simulation of origami, chemistry or mycelium
charges the digital simulator while deleting the physical cost claim. A real
physical comparison requires multiple substrates and hardware-specific I/O,
which violates source identity and is outside the current local apparatus.

## Observation, interpretation and uncertainty

**Observation.** Environment-mediated memory and physical computation are
real. Their observed benefits come from an explicit external channel, fixed
substrate dynamics or conventional transformation amortization. Existing
NEXTAI experiments already falsify learned variants of all three at their
declared software/full-cost boundaries.

**Interpretation.** Closed-loop environmental computation does not leave a
distinct causal thesis for NEXTAI. Moving state or dynamics across the
candidate/environment boundary changes accounting location, not the
computational factor. Physical reservoirs are a legitimate hardware program,
but not one source-identical observation-learned rule across unrelated tasks.

- Confidence `0.99` that digital stigmergy is external memory/communication.
- Confidence `0.98` that instance rearrangement is preprocessing,
  compilation, indexing or preconditioning under full accounting.
- Confidence `0.97` that the audited physical systems are fixed reservoirs
  with substrate-specific I/O and trained readouts.
- Confidence `0.95` that simulating those substrates locally cannot test their
  claimed physical cost advantage.
- Confidence `0.90` that no admissible fourth case exists in the current
  evidence; uncertainty remains for future real hardware with a genuinely
  shared raw interface.

## Decision

`REJECT_CLOSED_LOOP_ENVIRONMENTAL_COMPUTATION_AS_EXTERNAL_MEMORY_OR_SUBSTRATE_SPECIFIC_RESERVOIR`.

Do not create an environmental-memory benchmark, writable-world API, physical
simulator, hypothesis or score. Preserve the physical literature as evidence
that the system boundary matters, not as candidate evidence.
