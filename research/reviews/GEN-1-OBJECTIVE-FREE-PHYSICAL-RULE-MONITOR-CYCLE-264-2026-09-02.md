## Audited observations

### Magnetic physical self-learning

`SRC-0309` is the strongest empirical near-anomaly. A fabricated four-node
magnetic Hopfield device receives voltage patterns and its texture-defined
conductance matrix evolves without iterative digital weight updates. The
measured evolution is identified with Oja's rule; the device performs
associative recall and can be configured for Boolean logic.

This moves the *carrier* of the update into material physics but does not learn
the update law. Oja normalization and the energy landscape are fixed by device
physics, while voltage polarity is an authored pattern interface. The
experiment is one device; larger-scale memory is simulated. There is no
factor-off proof across an unrelated substrate and no full sensing, source,
instrumentation, training, inference, state and energy crossover. A digital
Oja/Hebbian control therefore already supplies the same causal rule.

### Marginally absorbing sphere-packing memory

`SRC-0310` finds that a modeled athermal disordered solid cyclically trained by
inverse design can retain the prescribed elastic-response range through a
marginally absorbing manifold. This is a useful physical-memory principle, but
the target elastic response and gradient are explicitly supplied and the work
is computational, not an empirical autonomous learner.

### Mechanosensitive cytoskeletal learning

`SRC-0311` derives a local strain-memory kernel from a coarse-grained model of
mechanosensitive proteins and motors. Persistent rest-length remodeling can
produce a desired mechanical response. The contrastive protocol, however,
applies an external supervised force proportional to error from a selected
target strain. The authors present empirical knockdown predictions as future
tests. This is fixed mechanochemical dynamics plus a supplied target, not an
observed objective-free learned rule.

### Intrinsic sensory dependencies

`SRC-0312` connects a simulated mechanically coupled skin to a simulated
cortical-like network with continuous independent Hebbian-like updates.
Random touch develops robust representations that support an unseen kinematics
readout. The result is intriguing objective-light representation learning, but
the Hebbian rule, sensor mechanics, wiring and interface are fixed by the
model; there is no empirical intervention, unrelated second substrate or full
cost boundary.

## Observation

Across the four sources, apparent objective-free learning always separates
into a fixed update law plus changing state. The magnetic device supplies the
best empirical evidence that state updates can occur intrinsically, but the
law is classical Oja learning. The other three studies are models whose target,
gradient, Hebbian rule or sensor coupling is specified by the researcher.

## Interpretation and confidence

Physical embodiment can remove a digital processor and can amortize a fixed
update, but it does not by itself create a new information-bearing edge or
make the rule observation-learned. This distinction prevents a causal-
equivalent implementation of Oja, Hebbian or contrastive gradient learning
from becoming a NEXTAI experiment merely because its state lives in matter.

Confidence is `0.995` that none of the four sources passes the prospective
gate, `0.99` that `SRC-0309` learns weights under a fixed rule rather than the
rule, and `0.97` that the remaining studies lack empirical factor-specific
cross-substrate evidence. Confidence is only `0.72` that no future material
system will show endogenous rule discovery.
