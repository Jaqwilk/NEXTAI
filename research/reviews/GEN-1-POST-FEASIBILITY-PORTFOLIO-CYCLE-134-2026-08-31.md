# GEN-1 — post-feasibility portfolio review, cycle 134

## Scope

This was exactly one no-scoring primary-literature and portfolio review after
five consecutive development-only feasibility failures in cycles 129–133. It
created no hypothesis, experiment plan, seed, candidate, benchmark, schema or
protected evaluator change. It loaded no benchmark arrays or held-out targets,
did not invoke the audited scoring harness and did not change scientific
confidence. The active cohort remains
`heldout_three_family_continuous_transfer_v7`.

External scores are prior-art context only. The review asks whether one genuinely
different principle can already be discriminated in a frozen NEXTAI cohort at
three scales with matched classical controls.

## 1. What was objectively learned?

The five audits tested the remaining observable symmetry breakers rather than
new parameterizations. Candidate-visible auxiliary structure did not satisfy a
shared identifiability theorem (cycle 129); a sparse local dependency rule had
insufficient development advantage and no shared coordinates (130); masked
uncertainty did not predict useful extra computation beyond classical
certificates (131); deeper conditional structure worsened WT likelihood and
reduced hierarchy stability (132); and neither DronePropA nor WT contained
natural unlabeled persistent-entity views needed for identity acquisition (133).

Together with EXP-20260831-0009, these failures show that the frozen anonymous
continuous contracts can test regularization and within-world adaptation, but
cannot currently distinguish discovery of a portable ontology from implicit
family routing or evaluator-supplied alignment. No scored experiment is
scientifically justified under the current contracts.

## 2. Which assumptions were falsified?

- Anonymous channel exchangeability is not a sufficient observable bridge.
- A source-identical sparse graph, hierarchy, update rule or recurrent state does
  not become transferable merely because it is compact or permutation
  equivariant.
- Candidate-visible uncertainty is not automatically an adaptive-compute signal;
  it must predict marginal quality gain beyond residual and convergence controls.
- Ordinary supervised row alignment or temporal adjacency is not persistent
  identity supervision.
- Repeating the same missing-coordinate problem with a larger memory or deeper
  learner would not create identifiability.

## 3. Which results replicated?

The negative foreign-family conclusion has replicated across low-rank dynamics,
spectral summaries, bounded residuals, expert selection, predictive indices,
learned update laws and five subsequent feasibility gates. The only positive
three-seed result remains the WT-specific bounded recurrent residual; it did not
transfer across families. Classical persistence, RLS/ridge, autoregressive,
probabilistic-structure and exact controls remain the relevant matched baselines.

## 4. Is the portfolio trapped in one family?

The repository spans programs, graphs, compression, masked inference,
changepoints and continuous physical worlds, but the recent portfolio is trapped
by one observation contract: anonymous arrays without a naturally shared object,
intervention or algebra interface. More candidate architecture inside that
contract has low expected information gain.

The next useful move is therefore not another benchmark-specific algorithm. It
is to verify whether an existing real public dataset already exposes the missing
multi-view and interventional structure without requiring NEXTAI to invent an
ontology.

## 5. Are implementation details being optimized instead of principles?

They would be if work continued by changing rank, sparsity, depth, residual
bounds, memory size, learning rates or routing. The five preregistered gates
failed for contract-level reasons. This review authorizes no tuning and no
scoring rescue.

## 6. What result would most change current beliefs?

A high-information result would show one source-identical learner recovering a
portable operator from naturally synchronized real views and interventions,
with intervention or view randomization destroying the effect, while classical
CCA/ICA/PCA, clustering, adaptive linear, autoregressive and probabilistic
controls are matched on quality and fully charged cost. The same frozen rule
must work at three declared scales and transfer to unseen intervention or
environment combinations without semantic labels at inference.

## 7. Which prior work already contains the apparent novelty?

ATLAS (SRC-0174) expands test-time memory by optimizing against current and past
tokens, but a small local version collapses into replay, test-time optimization
and recurrence already tested here; an honest scaled comparison is outside the
current bounded cohorts. Ng et al. (SRC-0175) show that even a predictive Markov
blanket can be underspecified and obtain identifiability by partitioning causal
roles; those roles are not candidate-visible in v7. Wang and Wang (SRC-0176)
derive symbolic emergence under task-relevant group invariance, which is not
supplied by generic channel anonymization. Luketina et al. (SRC-0177) use
modality-specialized interfaces and a shared controller, violating the frozen
source-identical rule rather than solving it.

Gamella, Bing and Runge (SRC-0178) provide a more relevant negative benchmark:
`lt_crl_benchmark_v1` is a controlled real optical system with images, known
causal inputs and intervention protocols, yet representative CRL methods fail.
Its existence supports a contract audit, not an immediate new benchmark or a
claim that its factors may be exposed to the learner.

## 8. Candidate directions and decision

### A. Larger test-time memory — reject under current contracts

Capacity, replay horizon and update optimization would change together. Current
cohorts cannot isolate the claimed long-memory principle from simpler RLS,
recurrent and support controls at matched capability.

### B. Causal-role or modular-interface transfer — reject as specified

Supplying parent/child/spouse roles, modality names or per-family encoders would
add exactly the manual ontology the official question forbids. No frozen cohort
contains a source-identical way to learn those interfaces independently of test
labels.

### C. Existing real interventional multi-view contract — retain for one audit

`lt_crl_benchmark_v1` is not selected as a new benchmark. It is selected only as
the most plausible existing source of the missing observation structure. Before
download, migration or scoring, NEXTAI must establish which fields are learner
visible, whether image/factor/intervention records are synchronized, how train
and unseen environments are separated, and whether three meaningful scales and
ontology-free controls exist.

Decision: `no_scored_experiment_justified_under_current_frozen_contracts`.
Create no hypothesis and do not alter v7.

## Exact next discriminating cycle

Perform one no-scoring, metadata-and-paper-only
`lt_crl_real_multiview_contract_review`. Do not download the large image data,
read benchmark outcomes, create a cohort or implement a candidate.

The review must retain the dataset only if all of the following can be specified
from public immutable metadata before scoring:

1. synchronized candidate-visible views and intervention indicators are present,
   while ground-truth factor identities can be isolated as evaluator-only data;
2. train/development and unseen intervention/environment splits can be frozen
   without using outcome-dependent selection;
3. at least three scientifically meaningful data, intervention or environment
   scales exist without subsampling tricks that change the task;
4. view-breaking, intervention-shuffle and identity/permutation negative controls
   are definable without semantic channel labels at inference;
5. PCA/ICA/CCA, clustering/record-linkage, autoregressive, adaptive-linear and
   applicable causal controls can share the same visible inputs and full-cost
   accounting;
6. the public data license, immutable checksum, compressed size and local storage
   budget are explicit;
7. no family-specific interface, path, variable name, ground-truth graph or
   simulator is needed by the learner; and
8. the proposed contrast measures transfer to unseen worlds rather than recovery
   of labels exposed during training.

If any condition fails, append `no_existing_real_multiview_contract` and do not
create a hypothesis. If all pass, request or use existing authorization for one
minimal protected service migration in a later wake; only a separate subsequent
wake may preregister a quick experiment.
