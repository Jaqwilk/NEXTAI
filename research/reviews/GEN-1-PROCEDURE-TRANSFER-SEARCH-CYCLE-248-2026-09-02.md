The window contains three scientifically valid scored mechanisms:

1. `EXP-20260901-0060` learned a particle proposal. Its K-independent query
   and slot-local update signatures held, but the learned proposal improved
   bootstrap by only `0.001570` NRMSE, lost `0.010621` to the matched posterior

2. `EXP-20260901-0062` learned input-dependent diagonal state retention. Its

3. `EXP-20260902-0001` learned anonymous local prolongation. All learned roles
   met residual quality with `5/1/5` iterations, but every shared-versus-
   independent and cross-family-versus-support transfer margin failed. The

   Its smaller residual is explained by extra target-local coarse capacity,
   not foreign-source information.

The common surviving observation is weak but precise: learned machinery can
change a surrogate, residual or local computational signature, yet the learned
state has not supplied a target-useful operation unavailable to the matched
classical/frozen control. In every G1 result the useful part was already
present in a posterior mean, disabled recurrence, PPM/CTW, adaptive SA or
target-only fit.

## CAUSAL NON-EQUIVALENCE AUDIT

Earlier NEXTAI update-rule tests do not close every procedure-transfer thesis:

- `EXP-20260830-0043` meta-selected a learning rate and surprise threshold for
  a fixed feature/state architecture. Shared lost to independent and fixed LMS
  by large capability and full-cost margins.
- `EXP-20260831-0009` transferred a six-bin normalized-LMS rate table while
  fixing the predictor and update equation. Shared beat independent, but
  cross-family-only lost to support-only in all nine cells and was unstable.
- Many later tests learned representations, routing scores, residuals,
  proposal weights, retention gates or prolongation labels inside a fixed
  human-specified computation.

A rule that generates prediction targets and their semantics, and thereby
changes the fast learner's update computation itself, is therefore not
causal-equivalent to those scalar/table/representation interventions. It is
also not automatically new: learned optimizers, learned objectives and
meta-learned local rules are established prior art. The only admissible NEXTAI
claim would be a source-identical cross-family causal and full-cost result,
not novelty of meta-learning.

## PRIMARY-LITERATURE CONTRADICTION

`SRC-0255` is the strongest positive contradiction. DiscoRL meta-learns a
function that maps trajectories of policies, predictions, actions, rewards and
termination indicators to targets for the policy and for predictions whose
semantics are not fixed in advance. The same rule transfers to unseen RL
benchmarks, observation/action spaces and agent architectures, and more varied
discovery environments improve held-out performance. This demonstrates that
procedure and prediction-semantics transfer can succeed where parameter or
representation transfer may fail.

The contradiction is conditional, not a NEXTAI result:

- the interface already assumes the RL ontology of action, reward, episode,
  policy and future trajectory;
- predefined action values and auxiliary policy predictions remain in most
  experiments;
- Disco57 discovery used `1024 * 64 = 65,536` TPUv3 core-hours and Disco103
  used `2048 * 60 = 122,880` TPUv3 core-hours;
- reported evaluation efficiency does not establish full-system crossover
  after charging discovery against a declared reuse horizon.

`SRC-0256` shows learned optimizers can generalize far beyond meta-training
scale, but the architecture mirrors known optimization structure. `SRC-0257`
is the necessary adversarial control: an independent evaluation of VeLO found
a task-specific hyperparameter, no consistent quality advantage and no faster
loss reduction after more than 4000 TPU months of training.

`SRC-0258` and `SRC-0259` show a complementary route to amortization: discover
an exact algorithm once, compile it into a small reusable program and remove
the neural search from deployment. Their tensor and assembly games, however,
provide domain-specific DSLs, symmetries, correctness verifiers and objectives.
`SRC-0260` similarly transfers one processor across many algorithms only after
CLRS supplies a unified, hand-aligned representation and intermediate traces.

## NEW CONCEPTUAL NEIGHBORHOOD

The selected neighborhood is **meta-discovered compact update procedures**:

> From a naturally shared predict-then-observe interface, learn a bounded rule
> that generates fast-learner targets or updates, then freeze/compile that rule
> so deployment uses one small source-identical procedure rather than carrying
> source representations or rerunning discovery.

This neighborhood attacks a different failure mode. Target-local state can be
new for every world; only the procedure for acquiring and updating it must
transfer. The qualitative signature is not marginal accuracy: foreign-source
experience must change the update trajectory relative to an identical
support-only rule, retain local bounded updates, work under a consistent
permutation of anonymous channels, and reach a prospective end-to-end
break-even after discovery is fully charged.

No experiment is authorized yet. The current evidence does not establish that
three qualitatively different frozen NEXTAI families expose a *natural* common
interface without evaluator-authored semantic roles. Nor does it show that a
locally discoverable rule has enough prospective reuse to recover meta-fit
cost. Implementing a meta-LSTM now would be architecture-first work and likely
repeat the VeLO cost failure.

## UNCERTAINTY AND CONFIDENCE

- Confidence `0.97` that the three G1 mechanisms supply no surviving learned
  causal gain or implementable Pareto evidence.
- Confidence `0.90` that full target/update-function discovery is causally
  broader than NEXTAI's prior rate-table and fixed-representation tests.
- Confidence `0.85` that a candidate-visible RL-style interface would import
  meaningful ontology unless every field is shown to arise naturally in the
  frozen families.
- Confidence `0.95` that discovery cost must be prospectively amortized and
  cannot be omitted because the compiled rule is cheap.
- Confidence only `0.25` that a bounded local version can beat strong classical
  rules under NEXTAI full-cost accounting; this is a search priority, not a
  proposed hypothesis.

## DECISION

`KEEP_SEARCH_SELECT_PROCEDURE_TRANSFER_NEIGHBORHOOD`.

Close learned prolongation as an immediate architecture source. Preserve the
new procedure-transfer neighborhood because primary evidence changes the broad
belief that only representations can transfer, but forbid a hypothesis or
scored quick until the interface and amortization admission gate passes. No
confidence or status of an existing hypothesis changes.
