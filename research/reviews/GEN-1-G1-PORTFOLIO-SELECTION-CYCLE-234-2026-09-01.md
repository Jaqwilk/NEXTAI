The one-time real-system calibration adds a different warning: cross-backend

## Existing-cohort result

No existing frozen cohort exposes a genuinely new mechanism together with a
source-identical causal ablation and strong classical controls:

- repository bytes expose sequence density, already covered by universal
  coding, grammar and recurrent/state contrasts;
- WT exposes local temporal adaptation, already covered by predictive state,
  particles, eligibility, sparse plasticity and adaptive filters;
- three-family continuous transfer exposes foreign-family transfer but has no
  legal common auxiliary variable;
- masked infilling exposes parallel and depth behavior, already covered by
  energy, tensor, grammar, pushdown and exact controls; the replicated pushdown
  rule failed its adversarial operation;
- whole-I/O and mechanism-recombination expose finite search only by supplying
  enough support for classical interpreters, or become nonidentifiable when
  that support is removed;
- entity/indexing cohorts are already explained by raw and classical indexes.

Freezing a role now would therefore spend a G1 item on a renamed prior
intervention. Creating a small target-shaped world would hide the missing
identifiability assumption in the generator. Both choices violate the window.

## Missing discriminator and decision

A valid new quick needs one observation-learned causal factor that is available
through a single anonymous interface, cannot be supplied by an existing
classical sufficient statistic, has a disabled or randomized causal ablation,
operates at three real scales, and exposes a transfer, local-update or
experience-dependent cost signature under full acquisition/fit/query/update/
state accounting. It must not depend on family labels, manual ontology,
generator metadata or hidden preprocessing. No frozen cohort currently
satisfies this conjunction for an untested mechanism.

Decision: `no_nonduplicate_existing_cohort_contract`. Do not create HYP-0059,
do not preregister EXP-0063 and do not mutate the active evaluator. This is a
portfolio selection result, not evidence against every future architecture and
not a reason to lower scientific gates.
