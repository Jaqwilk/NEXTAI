## Portfolio observation

The six-result window tested layer-local goodness, fixed recurrent state, local
sparse predictive codes, conditional execution and multiresolution lifting.
Several candidates learned something or reduced a narrow work component:
Forward-Forward reduced fit work, sparse routing halved active expert work,
predictive codes weakly improved over frozen code, and learned lifting beat its
frozen ablation and classical WT controls. None passed its source-identical
causal quality conjunction. PPM-D/CTW remained much stronger on bytes, and the
full lifting role was slightly worse than single-scale at equal aggregate cost.

This is not evidence that local learning, sparse computation or learned
operators are impossible. It is evidence that work reduction without a
source-identical quality effect is the recurring failure mode. Formal frontier
membership across many opposing axes must not override that causal result.

## Prior-art boundary

Growing Neural Gas and ART already cover incremental prototype/category
creation, so the initially proposed event-triggered operator dictionary would
mostly reopen failed routing, predictive-index and transition-bank families.
Predictive-state representations already ground latent state in future tests.
E-prop and predictive STDP show that local temporal plasticity is established;
locality alone earns no novelty claim and eligibility/event costs cannot be
hidden.

The distinct remaining question is narrower: can prediction error itself gate
both computation and strictly local plasticity, so activity and update traffic
fall as experience makes a stream predictable, without sacrificing held-out
quality? That principle is not the fixed reservoir, top-k expert router,
multiresolution transform or growing dictionary tested in this window.

## Selection and next cycle

Reject the growing operator-dictionary proposal before implementation. Select
one low-confidence breadth scout of an anonymous surprise-gated local
predictive-plasticity graph on the existing WT prequential data, subject first
to a minimal source-identical contract/versioning check. Freeze development-
only event encoding, residual threshold, edge cap, decay and update rule before
candidate scoring. Compare plastic event gating with dense-always-update and
frozen-plasticity ablations plus persistence, control-level/transition banks
and LMS/RLS. Charge event extraction, idle checks, eligibility/edge state,
updates, queries, bytes and full reuse workloads.

The qualitative gate must combine matched NRMSE and worst-case quality with
finite H96 extrapolation, local selected-edge mutation, K-independent state
cap, and lower event/update work in later public-support phases than earlier
ones. One seed is scout evidence only and cannot promote. A negative ends the
exact rule without threshold, edge-cap or decay tuning. Do not spend more than
one intervening no-scoring service/design wake; if the existing evaluator
cannot expose early-versus-late event work without changing completed metrics,
freeze the smallest append-only cohort version and score on the following wake.
