# GEN-1 — literature and portfolio review, cycle 42

Scope: mandatory review-only wake after 41 completed experiments, exactly six scored cohorts after the review at completed experiment 35. No experiment plan, benchmark/evaluator mutation, candidate code, scoring seed or runner call was created. Six primary records `SRC-0110`–`SRC-0115` were added.

## 1. What was objectively learned?

- `EXP-0038`: behavioral transfer across opaque program tokens and state conjugacies was possible, but exact relational graph MDL matched learned alignment at every capability/query metric and used less fit, state and R16 work.
- `EXP-0040`: samples supported exact context-specific circuit recovery, but learned decomposable SPN was behaviorally identical to contextual Chow–Liu and strictly more expensive. Structure discovery grew far faster than K while compiled queries stayed cheap.
- `EXP-0041` and `EXP-0042`: one pooled representation over four existing families did not transfer. Repairing output permutation equivariance raised accuracy from `0.0156` to `0.1771`, but minimum-family accuracy remained zero; a shared empirical control was better and cheaper, while contextual specialists reached `0.9792`.
- `EXP-0043`: a shared meta-learned online update scored `0.05455`, lost to its independently fitted ablation and fixed LMS, and paid `109.73x` LMS R16 work, `59,520x` meta-fit and `36.85x` state. The segmented oracle remained exact.
- `EXP-0044`: on immutable real repository files, validation-weighted hierarchical compression reached `4.32759` bits/byte, but a simpler context-tree mixture reached `4.24489`, with better worst-file and cold loss, equal state, `8.41x` less meta-fit and lower R16 work.
- Across the whole ledger there are 41 completed results, 35 benchmark versions, 33 quicks and 8 screens. There are 289 complete candidate outcomes, one crash and one timeout. Fifteen of sixteen architectural hypotheses are dormant; only skeptical accounting HYP-0012 remains testing.
- The stable positive is not a learned successor architecture. Indexing, compilation, adaptive halting, event queues, predictive quotients and sparse active execution can lower query work once a useful representation exists. No learned method has repeatedly acquired that representation across families and remained non-dominated after acquisition, fit, update, state and repeated-use cost.

## 2. Which assumptions were falsified?

- Pooling heterogeneous examples is not evidence of shared representation learning. EXP-0041/0042 showed that a family-neutral serializer and one parameter set can erase sufficient statistics while specialists remain capable.
- Equivariance repair is necessary but not sufficient for transfer. A correct atom-pointer head removed one implementation defect without producing a useful shared representation.
- Meta-learning a local update does not automatically outperform a fixed online estimator. The fit/update cost can exceed the adaptive baseline by orders of magnitude while quality falls.
- Real byte data alone does not protect against a classical sufficient-statistic collapse. HYP-0016 reduced to expensive variable-order context mixing and lost cold transfer to CTW.
- Tractable learned circuits, learned alignment, masked state, fast weights and motif hierarchies are not distinct principles when their observable algorithm is the same table, graph match, context mixture or regression update used by a simpler control.
- A favorable query-only slope remains insufficient. Representation discovery, raw input processing, state, memory traffic and updates repeatedly dominate the amortized boundary.

## 3. Which results replicated?

- All six post-review cohorts repeated the matched-capability classical-null pattern or failed before matching specialist capability.
- Exact or classical sufficient representations dominated learned versions in program alignment, probabilistic circuits, online estimation and real sequence compression.
- Cross-family transfer failed twice under the same frozen evaluator, including the preregistered repair of the only identified output-symmetry defect.
- The result now spans synthetic symbolic, probabilistic, continuous online and real byte-sequence settings. This broadens the empirical support for HYP-0012 as an accounting discipline, but still does not prove its universal open-world statement.
- No positive learned advantage has replicated across at least two families, three blinded seeds and a changed representation while surviving full-system Pareto accounting.

## 4. Is the portfolio trapped in one family?

Yes at the causal level. Surface families changed, but almost every experiment asked whether a learner could recover a small stationary sufficient statistic that a deliberately matched classical algorithm could also recover exactly. This produced rigorous nulls, but continuing the pattern would optimize the project's ability to confirm HYP-0012 rather than explore successors.

The next test must change the inference factorization, not merely the statistic. Dense autoregressive LLMs have a sequential output dependency even when their token-mixing operator is efficient. The portfolio has tested adaptive recurrent steps and compression, but has not directly measured whether many unknown output positions can be resolved in a bounded number of simultaneous refinement rounds at competitive total work.

## 5. Are we optimizing implementations rather than principles?

Further changes to EXP-0038, 0040, 0042, 0043 or 0044 would be implementation tuning and are prohibited by their analyses. Increasing rank, meta examples, optimizer richness, motif length, context depth or state merely moves within a falsified cohort.

Three genuinely different candidate directions were compared:

| Direction | Primary precedents | Discriminating signature | Review decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parallel masked denoising / iterative insertion | D3PM (`SRC-0110`), MaskGIT (`SRC-0111`), Levenshtein Transformer (`SRC-0112`) | conditional quality at sequential refinement depth R much smaller than masked span length L, with all parallel work charged | select for an identifiability/service gate |
| Learned model plus verifier/tree search | DreamCoder, MuZero (`SRC-0113`) | fewer verified search nodes across new domains | reject now: the ledger already tested program libraries/search, and local quick compute cannot match the self-play/search boundary |
| Structured SSM / long convolution | Mamba (`SRC-0093`), S4 (`SRC-0114`), Hyena (`SRC-0115`) | subquadratic context mixing at matched capability | reject as the next local test: these are established operator baselines and can retain autoregressive output depth |

The selected direction is not claimed as novel. Its value is a clean new causal axis—parallel output refinement—rather than another name for learned representation discovery.

## 6. What result would most change current beliefs?

A result would materially change beliefs if one unchanged masked learner, trained only on whole training files, reconstructed runner-masked spans in unseen files with:

- lower conditional bits/byte and higher exact-span rate than left-to-right CTW/PPM and a small autoregressive control;
- quality at least matching an exact finite-order bidirectional Markov forward-backward/Viterbi control;
- sequential inference depth bounded by registered rounds `R=1/4/6` rather than span length `L`;
- total acquisition, fit, all-position work, updates, state and R16 workload low enough to avoid Pareto dominance by the bidirectional classical control;
- a positive iterative-feedback ablation: the same learner with one independent masked pass must be worse on dependency-coupled spans;
- persistence across unseen files, byte relabelings and mask geometries without syntax, paths, ASTs or a hand-written ontology.

A negative would also be decisive: if exact bidirectional Markov/CTW or parallel belief propagation matches quality at lower full cost, then local masked refinement is another classical inference implementation and HYP-0017 becomes dormant without tuning.

## 7. Which prior work already contains the apparent novelty?

- D3PM already connects absorbing-mask corruption, discrete diffusion and text generation. Iterative masked byte denoising is not a NEXTAI invention.
- MaskGIT already generates many positions simultaneously and refines them across rounds. Any claim must separate critical-path rounds from total model evaluations and tokenization.
- Levenshtein Transformer already performs partially non-autoregressive insertion/deletion. Flexible editing is established prior art.
- Exact chain forward-backward, Viterbi, bidirectional n-gram models and parallel belief propagation are mandatory classical nulls even though they are not learned neural models.
- S4, Mamba and Hyena show that efficient sequence operators are mature. A tiny recurrent control is insufficient, and operator efficiency must not be confused with non-autoregressive decoding.
- MuZero and DreamCoder show that learned models can guide search, but neither makes simulator calls, search nodes, generated experience or model evaluations free.

## 8. Which next test has the highest expected information gain?

Create HYP-0017 at low confidence and use the next wake only for a no-scoring mathematical/service gate for `heldout_parallel_masked_infilling_v1`. Do not reuse the EXP-0044 test cohort for claims or tune from its cells.

### Identifiability gate before evaluator construction

1. Freeze a new whole-file corpus from repository files excluded from the EXP-0044 corpus; keep train/validation/test files disjoint by SHA-256 and record exact/near-duplicate checks. It remains visible local screening data, not an independent hidden benchmark.
2. Runner-random seeds choose test files, mask spans and a byte permutation only after candidate audit. Candidate fit sees train/validation bytes; an implementable query receives anonymous corrupted bytes and mask positions, never paths, targets or future refinement states.
3. One refinement round predicts every currently masked position from the same previous-round snapshot. Within-round teacher forcing or left-to-right target reveal invalidates the cohort. The candidate may reveal only its selected predictions between rounds.
4. Register span lengths independently of rounds, including `L > R`, so sequential depth cannot be hidden in per-position loops. Report critical-path rounds and total operations/bytes touched separately.
5. Required controls: uniform/unigram, left-to-right PPM and CTW, small dense autoregressive, exact finite-order bidirectional Markov forward-backward/Viterbi, parallel fixed Markov belief propagation, one-pass independent masked learner, iterative masked learner, and a privileged conditional oracle only if its complete cost is defined.
6. Full accounting includes corpus acquisition/corruption, fit and validation selection, every position-round probability, confidence sorting/reveal, update, state, bytes touched, total work, critical path and R1/R4/R16 reuse.
7. Quick success requires a registered quality margin over all implementable controls, a strictly positive iterative-feedback ablation, bounded critical depth as L grows, and implementable Pareto non-dominance on quality, total work, critical path and state. One seed can only discard or authorize a three-seed new-corpus screen.
8. Reject the benchmark before planning if fixed-order bidirectional Markov inference solves all spans exactly, if masked targets are conditionally unidentifiable, if files or masks leak through candidate-visible metadata, or if the runner cannot distinguish simultaneous rounds from a hidden sequential loop.

### Portfolio decision

- HYP-0016 remains dormant at `0.06`; do not tune the compression cohort.
- HYP-0012 remains testing at `0.80`. Append EXP-0038 and EXP-0040–0044 as relevant evidence, but do not raise confidence: five synthetic cohorts and one small visible repository still cannot justify a universal lower bound.
- Create HYP-0017, “Parallel masked refinement”, as `proposed` at confidence `0.16`. This confidence reflects a meaningful untested inference-factorization signature, not evidence that a local implementation will win.
- The next wake is service-only. It may freeze an evaluator only after all eight identifiability conditions pass; otherwise it must record rejection and select no substitute experiment in the same wake.
