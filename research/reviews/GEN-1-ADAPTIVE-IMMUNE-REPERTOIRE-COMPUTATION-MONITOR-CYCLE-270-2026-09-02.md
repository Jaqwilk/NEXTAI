# GEN-1 — adaptive immune-repertoire computation monitor, cycle 270

## Scope and prospective gate

This is one bounded no-scoring SEARCH MODE cycle. The gate in
`research/checks/adaptive_immune_repertoire_computation_monitor_cycle_270.json`
was frozen before source search. It asks whether a within-lifetime immune
repertoire learns one source-identical local update that transfers useful state
across unrelated antigen families, beyond inherited receptor generation,
mutation, selection, expansion, extra samples and antigen-specific intervention
design at full biological cost.

The gate inherits the cycle-252 result that survival and acceptance are
non-differentiable credit, not objective-free learning. It also excludes
associative memory, local plasticity, energy attractors, evolutionary search and
fixed random-feature or nearest-affinity repertoire aliases. This cycle creates
no hypothesis, experiment, plan, candidate, seed, score, benchmark or protected
apparatus change.

## Audited observations

### Germinal centres implement clonal selection and mutation

`SRC-0333` is the clearest mechanistic experiment. Photoactivation and altered
antigen presentation show that germinal-centre B cells presenting more antigen
to follicular helper T cells receive more rounds of division and somatic
hypermutation. High-affinity clones are therefore both expanded and diversified.

This is a powerful biological search process, but the causal rule is exactly
fitness-dependent population selection with mutation. The experiment does not
identify a second observation-learned update law, cross-family transfer of
learned state, or an advantage over an evolutionary-search control after the
cost of failed clones and cell divisions is included.

### Natural gut repertoires combine inherited bias and selection

`SRC-0334` profiles chronic Peyer's-patch germinal centres in mice. Faecal
transfer to germ-free mice restores microbiota-dependent public clonotypes,
directly implicating antigen-driven selection, and recurrent somatic mutations
show affinity maturation. However, the recurrent public clonotypes are also
strongly enriched by V(D)J junctional-generation biases in the naive repertoire.

Thus apparent reuse across animals is not a representation discovered from
unrelated exposures. A genetically supplied proposal distribution creates
frequent receptors, after which local antigens select and expand them. There is
no frozen-selection or matched-repertoire intervention isolating a portable
learned rule.

### Boosting produces breadth within one related virus family

`SRC-0335` longitudinally studies 42 people after three ancestral SARS-CoV-2
mRNA vaccine doses. The third dose expands persisting and newly detected memory
B-cell clones, and more than half of analysed neutralizing memory antibodies
after boosting neutralize Omicron despite its absence from the vaccine.

This is genuine OOD-variant breadth, but not the gate's cross-family transfer.
All inputs and tests remain within the SARS-CoV-2 RBD family and exploit
conserved epitopes. The observed change is explained by clonal expansion,
emergence and mutation; most already broad persisting clonal pairs changed
little in breadth. There is no source-identical comparison showing that state
learned from this family helps an unrelated antigen or task.

### Antibody feedback reshapes selection through supplied epitope masking

`SRC-0336` provides the strongest factor-specific intervention. People received
two high-affinity anti-RBD monoclonal antibodies before vaccination, with
supporting mouse experiments. Pre-existing antibodies altered memory-cell
specificity, lowered the affinity threshold for entry and increased low-affinity
IgM memory.

The intervention identifies direct epitope masking plus altered antigen
concentration and avidity. It does not reveal a learned general feedback rule:
the experimenter supplies antigen-specific antibodies that change which clones
survive within one RBD. Only one of 77 tested anti-RBD memory antibodies in the
intervention group neutralized virus, so diversification alone is not a
capability advantage.

## Observation

Adaptive immunity is a real lifelong memory system with local mutation,
selection and incremental response. It can generalize from an ancestral antigen
to unseen related variants. The audited causal evidence nevertheless assigns
the gain to a genetically generated diverse repertoire, affinity-dependent
clonal selection, somatic mutation, population expansion and antigen-specific
feedback. It does not show learned state transferring across unrelated families.

No source crosses the cycle-252 or cycle-261 boundary. The relevant computation
is evolutionary population search plus associative affinity, and its biological
cost includes generation and death of many clones, signalling, repeated
exposure and long-term memory maintenance. None of the studies reports a
matched-capability full-system crossover against that classical abstraction.

## Interpretation, uncertainty and confidence

The immune repertoire is evidence that a fixed local search rule can build
useful specialized memory without central retraining. It is not evidence for
the stronger NEXTAI target: a representation learned in one qualitative family
that improves computation in another. Related-variant breadth follows conserved
epitopes, while unrelated coverage comes primarily from the pre-existing naive
repertoire rather than transfer of learned antibody state.

A local software imitation would be an evolutionary algorithm over a supplied
affinity objective and candidate encoding. Without a new causal advantage over
frozen diversity, nearest-affinity kernels and matched population search, such
an experiment would rename known computation rather than change beliefs.

Confidence is `0.995` that none of `SRC-0333` through `SRC-0336` passes the
prospective gate, `0.995` that germinal-centre improvement is mediated by
selection, mutation and expansion, `0.98` that the gut public-repertoire result
depends materially on inherited generation bias, `0.99` that the booster result
shows related-variant rather than unrelated-family transfer, and `0.995` that
the antibody-feedback intervention supplies antigen-specific epitope masking.
Confidence is only `0.75` that no biological population learner could ever show
a full-cost algorithmic advantage after factor-specific controls.
