# Pre-seed parity/factor identifiability gate

The proposed sparse parity/factor graph was rejected before implementation.
The masked cohort applies an arbitrary runner-random permutation of all 256 byte
symbols. XOR and ordinary parity are not equivariant under an arbitrary element
of the symmetric group, so a true LDPC-style rule would require a privileged
numeric alphabet that the public contract intentionally removes.

Replacing XOR with learned pair-relation tables does not rescue a distinct
mechanism. It is observationally the v5 learned pairwise energy graph: both fit
byte-pair tables from the same raw files and repeatedly update masked variables
from neighboring table scores. Soft messages are already represented by the
mandatory `parallel_markov_bp_masked_byte` control. `EXP-20260901-0027` found
that v5 iteration worsened loss by 1.250047 bits/byte versus its one-sweep
ablation and lost heavily to unigram and PPM. Renaming that computation as
syndrome decoding would duplicate a falsified family and violate breadth mode.
