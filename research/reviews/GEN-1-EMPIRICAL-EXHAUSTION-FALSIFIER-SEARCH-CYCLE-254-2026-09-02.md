## Strongest near-counterexample: ergodic sensor learning

Prabhakar and Murphey (`SRC-0281`) report one active exploration principle that
uses the learned conditional entropy of a sensor model as an ergodic coverage
distribution. It is applied without changing the exploration rule to simulated
electrosense and vision, and it improves learned reconstruction while using
less motion energy than random sampling and entropy-maximizing acquisition.
This is a real cross-modality qualitative result and the strongest falsifier
found in this cycle.

It nevertheless fails the admission contract for concrete causal reasons:

- the implementation assumes a controllable state `x`, action `u` and known
  dynamics `dx/dt = f(x,u)` rather than discovering an executable interface;
- electrosense uses a low-dimensional fully connected model while vision uses
  a CNN-CVAE over RGB pixels, so executable code and constants are not
  source-identical;
- there are two simulated sensing modalities, not three qualitatively
  different natural systems;
- the learned factor changes where data are acquired, making it active
  experimental design/ergodic optimal control rather than a new form of
  computation unavailable to frozen or classical controls;
- random and information-maximization comparisons are useful, but there is no
  ablation isolating a cross-system learned operator from the supplied
  state/action/dynamics interface; and
- energy of motion is reported, not conservative acquisition + model fit +
  control optimization + update + state + communication + reuse-horizon cost.

The paper therefore narrows the exhaustion claim: a generic acquisition rule
can transfer across substantially different sensors. It does not provide the
observation-learned, ontology-free, source-identical cross-family computation
or full-cost crossover required by NEXTAI. The active-acquisition causal class
is already represented in the ledger and cannot be reopened by this result.

## Other empirical falsifiers

### Regenerative positional memory

Raz et al. (`SRC-0282`) find dynamically changing positional-control-gene
domains in muscle after injury and conserved muscle-guided patterning in acoels
and planarians separated by more than 550 million years. This is impressive
natural repair and cross-species conservation. It is not observation learning
by one executable rule: the positional genes, muscle tissue, developmental
signals and regeneration target are evolved molecular machinery. The systems
are homologous rather than three qualitatively unrelated interfaces, and the
paper supplies no computational full-cost comparison.

### Distributed self-stabilizing synthesis

Faghih et al. (`SRC-0283`) automatically synthesize several distributed
self-stabilizing protocols with one SMT method. The method requires the network
topology, process variables, readable/writable neighborhoods and a temporal
logic specification. Its output is a finite protocol proven against those
inputs, not a rule learned from ordinary observations. The authors also note
high time and space complexity. This is strong program synthesis and formal
verification, but its supplied ontology and specification are precisely the
classical explanation.

### Automated scientific discovery

AI-Descartes (`SRC-0284`) combines symbolic regression with logical reasoning
and demonstrates the pipeline on three physical laws. Its inputs explicitly
include domain-specific axioms, named independent/dependent variables, an
operator subset, dimensional information, expression bounds and curated data.
KeYmaera X and Mathematica provide additional fixed reasoning machinery. The
result shows that theory can disambiguate data-compatible formulas; it is not
ontology-free discovery or one source-identical learner over unrelated natural
interfaces, and no matched-quality full end-to-end cost crossover is shown.

### Artificial life and open-endedness

The cycle-253 primary sources remain the relevant falsifiers. MODES

metrics, while Hintze (`SRC-0280`) shows that satisfying nominal open-endedness
criteria need not produce the intended complexity. No new primary system found
in this cycle removes the external capability criterion or supplies the other

## Observation, interpretation, confidence and decision

**Observation.** Four new primary systems and the cycle-253 artificial-life
controls expose valuable phenomena—cross-modal active acquisition, natural
regeneration, automatic distributed protocol construction and theory-guided
law discovery. Every executable case relies on supplied state/action dynamics,
topology and temporal specification, or domain axioms/operator vocabularies.
The biological case is evolved homologous machinery rather than an
observation-learned source-identical algorithm. None reports the required
full-system crossover.

**Interpretation.** The empirical search falsifies an overly broad statement
that generic rules never transfer: ergodic sampling transfers across two
sensor modalities. It does not falsify the narrower cycle-253 conclusion under
the NEXTAI conjunction. Constructing an evaluator around any near miss would
author the missing interface and make the result circular.

- Confidence `0.99` that none of the audited publications independently passes
  all seven gates.
