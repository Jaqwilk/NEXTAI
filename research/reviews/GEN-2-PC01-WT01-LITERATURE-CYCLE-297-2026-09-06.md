## Portfolio interpretation

The PC-01 sequence repaired measurement instrumentation and then produced three
valid final replicas of a conventional learned positive control. This makes a
future failure of a candidate more interpretable, but does not itself advance a
candidate architecture.

WT-01 isolates a real descriptive recurrence effect on two visible development
recordings: aggregate NRMSE improved by 0.1627937252458549 and the sign was
positive on each file. It simultaneously eliminates the strongest novelty
interpretation because the classical controlled VAR(2)/ARX form is numerically
equivalent. Recursive query work also rises substantially, so the experiment
does not establish economic dominance.

The external-audit warning remains decisive: permutations, K/H cells and
episodes do not create independent physical replications. Files 8-9 were already
visible historical diagnostics, and `wt_walks_v1` changes the data-generating
operation. Neither can support a same-protocol replication claim.
