# GEN-1 breadth portfolio reflection — cycle 226

This mandatory twelve-result reflection is a service-only cycle after

## 1. What was objectively learned?

The only independently replicated positive signature was the unchanged
observation-induced pushdown learner in EXP-0042: across three seeds, a
disjoint source corpus and unseen depths, it solved all 144 spans exactly,
while source-identical finite-state ablations remained at zero and the best
completed implementable control reached only 0.0347 mean exact-span accuracy.
The result is narrow rather than promotable. The adversarial recoverable-push
cohort in EXP-0044 reduced the same learner to zero on all 144 spans, showing
that it learned a closure-chain operation rather than a reusable bidirectional
operator.

The remaining valid screens supplied useful diagnostics but no promotable
mechanism. Learned exact-search guidance in EXP-0045 and EXP-0059 preserved
exactness and reduced raw search versus enumeration, but lost to frozen
source-identical guidance after fit, certification, state and repeated-work
costs. Local continuous learners in EXP-0048, EXP-0049 and EXP-0051 obtained
sparse updates or near-zero knowledge/query slopes, yet their quality signal
was absent, below the frozen meaningful effect, or worse than ridge and aligned
ablations. Learned addressing in EXP-0054 and EXP-0056 achieved local insertion
but was worse than raw nearest-neighbour or k-d controls and retained total-cost
knowledge slopes near 0.66--0.72. Cross-family sparse set memory in EXP-0057
failed transfer in every family while contextual Chow--Liu remained the sole
high-quality Pareto point.

## 2. Which assumptions were falsified?

This window falsifies the assumption that a low query-time K slope, local
updates, sparse access, exact fallback, or learned search ordering is sufficient
evidence of a better computational principle. Full acquisition, fit,
verification, state and repeated workload frequently erased the apparent
advantage. It also rejects the exact frozen rules of HYP-0051 through HYP-0056;
none may be reopened by changing rank, shrinkage, chart coordinates, router
thresholds, memory sparsity, pattern count, branch order or certificate.

## 3. Which results replicated?

EXP-0042 replicated the stack-depth signature across three runner seeds and a
disjoint corpus. EXP-0044 then supplied a valid adversarial failure, so the
replication supports only forward closure-chain depth extrapolation. Across the
other families, the repeated result is negative: simpler source-identical or
classical controls retain the quality/cost frontier. Single-seed cells and K/D
conditions are not counted as independent replications.

## 4. Is the portfolio trapped in one family?

No by surface family: the window spans masked symbolic inference, exact program
search, real temporal prediction, nonlinear local dynamics, learned indexing,
entity association and four-family transfer. It is nevertheless trapped in a
recurring intervention: learn a small selector, address or representation and
compare it with a frozen or classical sufficient statistic. That intervention
has now failed strongly enough that another nearby selector is low information.

## 5. Are we optimizing implementation details rather than principles?

Often yes. The last screens increasingly changed charts, routers, sparse slots,
search bounds and role contracts while preserving the same causal question.
Those implementation details produced auditable nulls but no general mechanism.
The correct response is portfolio deduplication and a genuinely different
causal intervention, not another repair of a closed family.

## 6. What result would most change current beliefs?

A belief-changing result would use one source-identical learner across unseen
worlds and beat the strongest raw, frozen and classical control at matched
quality and full R1/R4/R16 cost across at least three seeds and three knowledge
scales. It should also show one qualitative signature unavailable to the
control, such as successful transfer beyond trained computation depth or
experience-dependent reduction in inference work, while surviving an
adversarial operation rather than only a relabeling.

## 7. Which prior work already contains the apparent novelty?

Visibly pushdown automata already supply stack-mediated depth generalization;
pattern databases and branch-and-bound supply certified search guidance;
k-d trees and nearest-neighbour methods supply hierarchical addressing; and
Chow--Liu, autoregressive and empirical-joint models supply classical
probabilistic structure. The recent diagnostics do not establish novelty over
those families. The exact active-sensing posterior-partition DAG suggested
after EXP-0056 is also not open: EXP-20260901-0031 already tested and falsified
it, as recorded by the cycle-221 portfolio correction.

Whole-I/O v5, sparse set memory v5, latent-entity addressing v2/v3, continuous
local v5/v6, WT local credit v4 and masked-inference v13 have all answered the
