reservoirs, predictive codes, conditional execution, multiresolution lifting,
tensor-network inference, partial evaluation, and continuous cellular learning.

component-level signature—bounded state, K-independent query work, sparse
updates, lower dependency depth, small warm reuse, or exact output-preserving
scheduling—but fail to earn representation quality and full end-to-end cost at
the same time. Classical controls explain the useful component more cheaply.

affine ridge. Therefore scheduling should remain infrastructure/control prior
art, not be promoted as a learned successor mechanism.
