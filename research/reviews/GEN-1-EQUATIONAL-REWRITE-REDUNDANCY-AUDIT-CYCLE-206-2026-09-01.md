# Equational-rewrite redundancy and identifiability audit — cycle 206

## Scope

This is one service-only audit after valid negative EXP-20260901-0045. It
creates no hypothesis, experiment, plan, scoring seed, candidate, evaluator
version, or scientific evidence. The proposed direction was a source-identical
bidirectional learner that would infer cancellation/involution equations and
normalize anonymous length-4/8/16 operator compositions on the already frozen
`heldout_mechanism_recombination_v6` cohort.

## Observations

1. `operator_algebra_completion` already implements the relevant causal
   primitive. It infers error-free observable equations of the form
   `result = second(first(source))`, performs unanimous bidirectional closure,
   and is paired with source-identical `operator_algebra_no_relations` and
   `operator_algebra_independent` ablations.
2. Valid EXP-20260830-0057 isolated that primitive. The semantic fixture proved
   that relation closure added transitions unavailable to direct composition,
   and the no-relations role failed the fixture. The learned closure improved
   mean accuracy to 0.625 from 0.041667 for no-relations/exact-MDL, but minimum
   accuracy was 0.0, the registered quality gates failed, meta-fit cost was
   135671 versus 5192 for exact MDL, and no implementable role entered the
   capability-gated frontier. The exact rule was discarded without tuning.
3. V6 does not hide symbolic primitive names from candidate-visible terms by
   requiring their recovery. Each leaf directly contains its full anonymous
   144-state table; reassociation, inserted identities, and optional fusion are
   encoding variants. `flatten()` removes identities, while exact interpretation
   and `canonical_table()` already compute the same operator without learned
   equations.
4. The v6 development gate exposes positive equivalence and negative
   pair-breaking examples, but no cancellation or involution target. An exact
   enumeration over A/B/C for seeds 1501103, 734827148, 405583788, and
   808114446 found no `X^2 = I` and no `XY = I` relation. The held-out programs
   are fixed CBAC, CBACABAC, and CBACABACBCABCBAC. Therefore a learner whose
   only distinctive operation is cancellation/involution either learns no rule
   on v6 or must receive a new evaluator-supplied relation.
5. Valid EXP-20260901-0003 and EXP-20260901-0021 already tested learned operator
   reuse on the same public term/table boundary. Counterexample fingerprints and
   persistent macro DAGs were exact, but exact interpretation dominated their
   full cost or defeated their scaling gates. V6 also already freezes exact-key,
   structural-result, canonical-table, anti-unification, nearest-canonical, and
   exact-interpreter controls.

## Interpretation and uncertainty

The proposed learner is not a new causal test on the frozen cohort. Its general
equation-closure component aliases the already falsified operator-algebra rule;
its narrower cancellation/involution component has zero positive instances in
the checked v6 worlds. Adding such relations would change the data-generating
process specifically to make the candidate applicable and would require a new
benchmark rather than a role-only activation.

Confidence is 0.99 that role-only activation on v6 would be observationally
redundant or vacuous. The remaining uncertainty is limited to other operator
distributions containing genuine algebraic relations; this audit does not
claim that learned rewriting is universally useless.

## Decision

Reject the proposed v6 equational-rewrite direction before implementation.
Do not add role identifiers, candidate code, a cohort version, a hypothesis, a
plan, a seed, or scoring. This preserves the negative result without turning a
methodological review into a rescue benchmark.

This is consecutive no-scoring cycle 1 of the user-authorized maximum 3 after
scored cycle 205. It made a concrete pre-implementation rejection; cycle 207
must return to scoring rather than continue auditing.
