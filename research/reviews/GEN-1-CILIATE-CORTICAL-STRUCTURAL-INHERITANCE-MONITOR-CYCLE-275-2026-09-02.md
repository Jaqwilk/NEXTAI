# Ciliate cortical structural-inheritance monitor — cycle 275

## Scope and prospective boundary

This SEARCH MODE cycle created no hypothesis, experiment plan, scoring seed,
candidate, benchmark or score and changed no protected apparatus. The admission
contract was frozen in
`research/checks/ciliate_cortical_structural_inheritance_monitor_cycle_275.json`
before new sources were inspected, at source cutoff `SRC-0357`.

The gate deliberately distinguishes inheritance from learning. A qualifying
source had to show a transiently written spatial state copied through divisions,
factor-specific separation of write/copy/read, and improvement on an unseen
unrelated morphogenetic function by the same local rule. Copying an imposed
geometry, returning to the species body plan, or dynamically resisting current
mechanical force was insufficient.

## Objective observations

### `SRC-0358` and `SRC-0359`: real non-DNA inheritance, imposed state

Beisson and Sonneborn use conjugant surgery and heteropolar cortical fusion to
introduce reversed-polarity rows into *Paramecium*. During subsequent divisions,
the grafted rows extend and can form complete reversed rows. Nuclear-genetic
explanations are strongly constrained by coexistence of normal and reversed rows
in the same cell and by crosses. Some variants are lost, while selected complete
doublets and reversed rows can persist for very long lineages.

Nanney independently shows that *Tetrahymena* cortical row-number differences
persist longer than dilution of a passive cytoplasmic molecule. The values also
drift toward genotype-dependent stability centers. Together these papers prove
that a cell's existing geometry carries causal hereditary information, but the
stored state is imposed or sampled and then recopied. It is not inferred from
unlabeled observations and is not tested on an unrelated function.

### `SRC-0360`: strongest relational effect remains a fixed local-coordinate rule

Ng and Frankel rotate longitudinal ciliary rows by 180 degrees. New cortical
structures generated during growth reproduce the inverted orientation. More
importantly, the normally left-sided contractile-vacuole pore appears on the
right of an inverted row. Thus local row polarity causally determines a
different adjacent organelle's position.

This is more than passive photographic copying and is the strongest anomaly in
the audit. It nevertheless fails the frozen gate: the experimenter directly
authors the coordinate transformation, and the downstream effect follows a
fixed rule tied to the two sides of the row. There is no training episode,
unseen-task improvement, cross-family source identity, three-scale test or full
cost comparison.

### `SRC-0361` and `SRC-0362`: cortical boundaries call an evolved program

Tartar's graft experiments show that artificially constructed stripe-contrast
boundaries can act as organizers for oral primordium formation in *Stentor*.
De Terra strengthens the causal geometry: interposing an inverted cortical ring
creates permanent heteropolar discontinuities and consistently initiates basal
body assembly and oral development, whereas homopolar grafts that heal rarely
do so.

These are compelling local relational triggers, but the surgery supplies the
target boundary. The response is a species-specific oral-development program,
not a representation learned from earlier experience and reused for a new
function. Persistence through several divisions and complete end-to-end cost
are not jointly evaluated.

### `SRC-0363`: Mob1 isolates global pattern maintenance, not a learned write

Slabodnick et al. introduce RNAi into *Stentor* and show that asymmetric Mob1 is
needed for proportional shape, a single anterior-posterior axis and normal
regeneration. Mob1 depletion produces progressive elongation and medusoid cells
with multiple posterior-like poles. Tubulin, LF4 and absent-gene RNAi controls
help separate cortical integrity and nonspecific RNAi effects.

This identifies evolved molecular machinery required to execute and maintain a
body plan. It does not isolate a transient experience-dependent write, the
copying of that acquired state, or transfer between unrelated morphogenetic
functions.

### `SRC-0364`: reversible mechanical adaptation without inherited learning

Soh et al. provide modern factor-specific causal evidence. *Tetrahymena*
striated fibers change length with ciliary force, connect basal bodies to their
neighbors and cortex, and support reorientation. DisAp restoration lengthens
fibers before reorientation, while Cro1 loss specifically blocks force-induced
elongation and rescue restores the phenotype.

The state tracks present ciliary force and can reverse when force changes. It is
homeostatic mechanosensing, not a history-specific state copied through cell
divisions or a rule transferred to an unrelated task.

## Cross-source synthesis

The evidence separates into three computational classes:

- local self-templating of an imposed spatial orientation or count
  (`SRC-0358`–`SRC-0360`);
- fixed relational boundary detection that invokes an evolved morphogenetic
  program (`SRC-0360`–`SRC-0362`);
- molecular homeostasis maintaining a species-typical axis or ciliary array
  (`SRC-0363`, `SRC-0364`).

No source jointly shows observation-based writing, factor-specific write/copy/
read isolation, source-identical transfer to an unseen unrelated function,
three scales and full cost. A software translation would be a fixed local
cellular automaton, coordinate-frame propagation, boundary detector or feedback
controller. Those classical explanations already predict the qualitative
results without a learned representation.

## Interpretation and confidence

Ciliate cortex is decisive evidence that hereditary information is not limited
to DNA sequence. The gap to NEXTAI is not persistence or locality but learning:
the system perpetuates a supplied geometry or maintains an evolved body plan.
The contractile-vacuole result demonstrates relational reuse, yet the relation
is fixed rather than acquired from data.

- Confidence `0.995` that none of the seven sources passes the prospective gate.
- Confidence `0.995` that reversed-row inheritance is genuine non-genetic
  spatial information.
- Confidence `0.98` that the contractile-vacuole effect is a fixed local
  coordinate rule rather than learned cross-task abstraction.
- Confidence `0.98` that Stentor graft effects are experimenter-authored
  organizer boundaries invoking evolved repair/development.
- Confidence `0.99` that Mob1 and striated-fiber results establish patterning
  and homeostasis, not an experience-written transferable representation.
- Confidence `0.90` that this route is exhausted absent a primary experiment
  with acquired cross-function gain and explicit write/copy/read controls.
