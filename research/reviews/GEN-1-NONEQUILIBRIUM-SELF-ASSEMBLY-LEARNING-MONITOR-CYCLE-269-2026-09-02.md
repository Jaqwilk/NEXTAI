## Audited observations

### Physical DNA-tile nucleation recognizes patterns but does not learn in situ

`SRC-0329` is the strongest empirical result. A system of 917 designed DNA
tiles competitively nucleates three alternative assemblies. Concentration
patterns encode 18 grayscale `30 x 30` images; after a 150-hour anneal,
fluorescence and AFM measurements correctly classify the trained images and
probe robustness on variations.

The physical substrate performs inference, not autonomous learning. The paper
states that decision boundaries were trained in silico by changing the mapping
between image pixels and fixed molecular components. The DNA interactions and
three target assemblies are designed beforehand. A possible physical Hebbian
mechanism based on proximity ligation is described as future work, not
implemented or tested. There is consequently no learned-interaction ablation,
source-identical cross-family transfer or matched full-cost comparison.

### Chemical reaction network ensembles remain a theoretical construction

`SRC-0330` develops a general architecture in which detailed-balanced chemical
reaction network ensembles implement gradient descent on relative entropy. It
also derives thermodynamic learning costs and tradeoffs. This is useful theory
and is accompanied by simulation code, but no physical autonomous-learning
experiment demonstrates the proposed chemistry, much less unchanged transfer
across unrelated families with a factor-specific control and full measured
cost.

### Multifarious assembly is fixed associative memory

`SRC-0331` stores multiple structures by superposing designed pairwise binding
interactions among shared components. A desired structure is retrieved using a
seed or selected chemical potentials. The authors explicitly connect the
energy construction to a Hopfield associative memory. The structures are
therefore supplied in the interaction matrix before retrieval; observations do
not teach a local update rule. This is an important fixed control already
excluded by the prospective gate.

### Reinforcement learning controls assembly externally

`SRC-0332` trains neural-network policies by evolutionary reinforcement
learning on molecular simulation trajectories. The policies change temperature
and chemical potential to promote an authored target order parameter or choose
between polymorphs. The result is simulated external digital control, not a
physical assembly system learning its interactions from unlabeled exposure.
The reward, controller and target identity also prevent a source-identical
cross-family interpretation.

## Observation

Self-assembly can physically realize a high-dimensional fixed decision surface,
and chemical-network theory can express a learning objective. The audited
literature does not join those facts into the required experiment. In the one
strong physical demonstration, task learning remains an external in-silico
mapping over fixed interactions. The alternatives either encode memories in a
designed energy matrix or train an external rewarded controller in simulation.

No source adds information beyond the cycle-261 causal boundary. The apparent
capability is attributable to supplied molecular ontology, fixed pair

extra simulated trials rather than an observation-learned portable rule.

## Interpretation, uncertainty and confidence

Non-equilibrium nucleation changes where an inference is executed, but not who
learns the distinctions. Synthesizing tile identities, target assemblies and a
pixel-to-molecule map embeds the useful ontology before the sample enters the
physical system. Implementing a local software surrogate would collapse to the
associative-energy, reservoir or externally optimized controllers already
excluded, while claiming a molecular effect requires a new physical experiment
that the local harness cannot supply.

Confidence is `0.995` that none of `SRC-0329` through `SRC-0332` passes the
prospective gate, `0.995` that `SRC-0329` performs physical inference with
fixed interactions and in-silico training, `0.99` that `SRC-0330` lacks a
physical learning demonstration, `0.995` that `SRC-0331` is a designed
associative energy memory, and `0.995` that `SRC-0332` is external rewarded
simulation control. Confidence is only `0.70` that a future proximity-ligation
or other in-material experiment cannot satisfy the gate.
