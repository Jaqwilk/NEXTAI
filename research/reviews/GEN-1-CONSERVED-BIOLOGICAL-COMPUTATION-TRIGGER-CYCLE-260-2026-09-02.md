## Primary-source audit

### Fold-change detection — conserved behavior, not a learned implementation

Shoval et al. show fold-change detection in bacterial chemotaxis and formalize
the scalar symmetry that makes search behavior invariant to rescaling of a
sensory field. Goentoro and Kirschner independently show that beta-catenin
fold-change, rather than its absolute level, predicts Wnt outputs in mammalian
cells and Xenopus embryos. The latter experiments perturb pathway components
and reveal a bounded robust region.

This is the strongest cross-system quantitative analogy, but it fails the
causal claim. The systems use different molecules, state variables and network
structures. The Wnt paper explicitly reports that its existing kinetic pathway
model explains the effect without a new molecular learning mechanism. In
bacterial chemotaxis, Yi et al. derive robust perfect adaptation as integral
feedback structurally embedded in the receptor methylation network. These are
evolved, hardwired response circuits, not one within-lifetime rule learned from
observations. They also provide no common factor-off rescue, held-out learning
comparison or complete energy-state-time crossover.

Gate vector: `Y, Y, N, N, N, N, N, N, N`.

### Habituation in slime mould and sensitive plant — behavioral analogy only

Boisseau et al. repeatedly expose Physarum to quinine or caffeine bridges and
report response decline, spontaneous recovery, specificity and dishabituation.
Gagliano et al. report declining leaf-folding under repeated drops in Mimosa,
with environmental dependence and persistence. These are independent natural
systems and within-lifetime behavioral changes.

Neither source identifies a quantitative intracellular state-update law, much
less the same law, constants and update order in both systems. Neither removes
and rescues a molecular factor carrying the update. Their stimulus, response
and success semantics are selected by the experimenters, and no comparison to
strong classical adaptation models or complete cost boundary is available.
The common word “habituation” therefore does not establish source identity.

Gate vector: `Y, Y, N, Y, N, N, N, N, N`.

### Physarum transfer and anticipation — stronger behavior, one substrate

Vogel and Dussutour demonstrate that a habituated Physarum can transfer its
response to a naive fusion partner and that transfer requires sustained fusion.
This is a real state-transfer intervention. The paper does not identify the
transferred molecular state or update equation and explicitly leaves its form
for future work. Saigusa et al. report timed locomotor slowdowns after three
periodic unfavorable pulses, but the evidence is again behavioral and the
mechanism is explored by a dynamical model rather than factor-specific loss and
rescue.

Both results concern the same organism. They cannot establish independent
instantiation across unrelated systems, a common frozen update, or an energy
and reuse advantage. A software reproduction would impose a researcher-chosen
model on the behavior rather than test a biological causal factor.

Gate vector: `Y, N, N, Y, N, N, N, N, N`.

## Observation

The audited literature separates into two non-overlapping classes. Quantitative
cross-system regularities such as fold-change detection reduce to distinct
hardwired feedback or feed-forward circuits. Learning-like within-lifetime
behavior such as habituation and anticipation lacks an identified causal state
update, source identity across systems and full-cost measurements. No candidate
passes gates 3–5, and none approaches the cost gate.

This is a useful negative result: importing a verbal biological principle into
software would add ontology and model choice exactly where the natural evidence
is underspecified. It would not expose a non-equivalent computational factor.

## Interpretation, uncertainty and decision

Natural systems clearly adapt and store experience, but the sources do not
support the much stronger NEXTAI thesis of one independently instantiated,
observation-learned rule with causal and cost evidence across unrelated systems.
The direct biomimetic-universality route is closed under the frozen contract.

Confidence is `0.98` that fold-change detection here is not one source-identical
within-lifetime learner; `0.97` that the habituation sources do not identify a
common molecular update law; and `0.94` that no audited candidate supplies the
required complete cost boundary. Bibliographic incompleteness remains possible,
but it is not evidence for building apparatus.

Decision:
`CLOSE_DIRECT_BIOMIMETIC_UNIVERSALITY_ROUTE_NO_SOURCE_IDENTICAL_WITHIN_LIFETIME_RULE`.
