## Objective observations

### `SRC-0346`: Whi3 deceptive-courtship mnemon

Caudron and Barral provide the clearest within-cell conformational memory.
Prolonged pheromone exposure during an unsuccessful mating attempt slowly drives
polyQ/polyN-dependent Whi3 super-assembly, releases cell-cycle arrest and leaves
the mother refractory to pheromone. Hsp70 counteracts assembly, and the state is
largely retained in the mother rather than transmitted to all daughters.

This is a genuine factor-specific memory device, but it records one authored
pheromone-pathway event and changes the response to that same pathway. It does
not show transfer to an unrelated stress or function, three scales or complete
synthesis, maintenance, partition, reset and reuse cost.

### `SRC-0347` and `SRC-0348`: stress-modulated prion bet-hedging

Tyedmers et al. show that several stresses increase de novo `[PSI+]` formation,
in some cases by as much as 60-fold. Their matched mixtures also show that most
tested environments make `[PSI+]` neutral or harmful, so the effect is not
simply enrichment of a beneficial state during every exposure.

Halfmann et al. add strong natural and causal evidence. In about 700 wild yeast
isolates, Hsp104-dependent prions produce diverse phenotypes across carbon,
osmotic, oxidative, pH, drug and DNA-damage conditions. Guanidine, dominant-
negative Hsp104 and nonaggregating Sup35 or Mot3 controls causally remove many
effects. The same prion can help one phenotype and harm another, and its effects
depend strongly on genetic background.

Together these studies establish protein-state inheritance and adaptive
trade-offs, not experience-specific learning. Stress increases stochastic
switching among evolved states; the prion then exposes cryptic genetic variation
and population selection retains useful variants. The write does not infer a
portable rule from one family that improves an unseen unrelated family.

### `SRC-0349`: broad protein-based inheritance

Chakrabortee et al. transiently overexpress nearly every yeast ORF and identify
about 50 proteins that induce durable traits after expression returns to normal.
Protein-only transmission to naive cells is compelling evidence for a
non-genetic carrier. However, the experimenter chooses the ORF and imposes the
write through overexpression. The screen samples fixed heritable states; it does
not demonstrate unlabeled experience transfer, matched-abundance isolation or a
full-cost advantage.

### `SRC-0350`: Pub1 condensate material states

Kroschwald et al. show that starvation and heat create Pub1 condensates with
different liquid, gel-like or solid-like properties. These material states alter
cell-cycle arrest and recovery kinetics, and heat-induced states require
chaperones for disaggregation. Release from stress coincides with dissolution
over minutes or hours. This is causal adaptive phase behavior during stress, not
a persistent post-exposure memory that improves an unrelated function.

### `SRC-0351`: abundance-based cross-stress memory

Guan et al. provide a useful alternative explanation. Salt-pretreated yeast
retain hydrogen-peroxide resistance through divisions, including in daughters
that did not experience salt. The carrier is long-lived catalase Ctt1 synthesized
during pretreatment and partitioned to daughters. This is real cross-stress
memory, but explicitly persistent enzyme abundance and generic detoxification,
not conformational conversion or an acquired operator.

## Cross-source synthesis

The audited effects resolve into four known computational classes:

- a stimulus-specific molecular latch (`SRC-0346`);
- stress-modulated sampling and selection over evolved bistable states
  (`SRC-0347`, `SRC-0348`);
- experimenter-induced sampling of fixed protein states (`SRC-0349`);
- contemporaneous phase response or slowly diluted protein abundance
  (`SRC-0350`, `SRC-0351`).

No source jointly provides within-lifetime unlabeled writing, a factor-specific
erase that preserves nonconformational exposure effects, source-identical
cross-family transfer, three scales and a matched full-cost crossover. Combining
the persistence of one paper with the multi-condition phenotypes of another
would not establish one causal mechanism.

## Interpretation and confidence

Protein assemblies are genuine biological memory carriers. The missing property
is not persistence but abstraction: the observed state either remembers the
same cue, exposes a pre-existing genetic response repertoire, or preserves a
generic protective molecule. None learns an operator reusable on a new family.

- Confidence `0.995` that none of the six sources passes the prospective gate.
- Confidence `0.99` that Whi3 is causal memory but stimulus-specific.
- Confidence `0.98` that `[PSI+]` evidence supports bet-hedging and selection,
  not directed cross-family learning.
- Confidence `0.97` that Pub1 is adaptive phase response without durable
  post-exposure transfer.
- Confidence `0.95` that Ctt1 explains its reported memory by abundance rather
  than a conformational carrier.
- Confidence `0.90` that this audited route is exhausted absent a new primary
  factor-off cross-family experiment.
