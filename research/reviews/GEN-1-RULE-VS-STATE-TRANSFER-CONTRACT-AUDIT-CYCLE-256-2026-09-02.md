## Three claims that must not be conflated

1. **L1 — rule universality:** one exact learning rule and constants are
   independently initialized and trained in each family, and the learned
   factor causally beats frozen/classical controls in at least two families.
2. **L2 — representation transfer:** pooled learned state improves unseen
   worlds over the same rule fitted independently.
3. **L3 — foreign-source causal gain:** data from other families improves a
   target over target/support-only learning.

L3 implies a strong form of L2, but failure of L2 or L3 does not logically
falsify L1. Conversely, using the same optimizer name in several domains does
not establish L1 unless the complete rule is source-identical and its learned
factor beats matched controls in each domain.

## Contract comparison

The fixed G1 decision rule in AGENTS requires one observation-learned,
source-identical mechanism with causal gain at matched useful quality in at
least two frozen families or tasks, three seeds, an adversarial operation,
qualitative signature, full-cost Pareto non-dominance and no manual ontology.
It does not explicitly require foreign-source data to help.

The Scientific Protocol's cross-family-transfer sections intentionally make a
stronger claim: a pooled learner must beat an independent/no-cross-family
ablation, and later continuous cohorts also require cross-family-only to beat
support-only. Those are correct gates for L2/L3 claims, but they are not a
logical definition of every possible universal computational principle.

Therefore the cycle-255 anomaly is real at the contract level. The empirical
question is whether it changes the portfolio conclusion.

## Closest apparent counterexamples

### HYP-0028

EXP-20260831-0006 and unchanged three-seed EXP-20260831-0007 show a large,
stable and full-cost Pareto-nondominated WT improvement, bounded state, local
updates, zero K query slope and H96 depth extrapolation. This remains the best
single-family diagnostic in the ledger.

It does not establish L1. All three seeds use the same two files and change
only anonymous channel permutation. In EXP-20260831-0008, the independently
fitted same rule has NRMSE 62–253 on the two mechanical families and about
1.26–1.29 on continuous-event, while persistence is about 0.71–1.17. The shared
role also loses to persistence in eight of nine cells. Moreover, the WT plan
did not isolate recurrence from slot-local RLS and explicitly names
DMDc/VAR/system identification as a complete possible classical explanation.

### HYP-0025 and HYP-0029

Both show that pooling related same-family data can regularize a small rule.
HYP-0025 improves family-balanced average but worsens the worst family and is
slightly more expensive than persistence. HYP-0029 has positive
shared-versus-independent contrast in all cells but only a sub-threshold
aggregate improvement over persistence, cell regressions and higher R16 cost.
These are evidence for ordinary pooling/regularization, not one learned factor
that independently wins in multiple families.

## Single-family qualitative signatures

Several L0 mechanisms retain real diagnostics: sparse event updates reduce
work (HYP-0031), sparse cellular execution and dyadic lifting reduce query
depth (HYP-0039/HYP-0046), and bounded recurrent residuals work on WT
(HYP-0028's initial cohort). In every case either useful quality loses to a
simpler control, the learned factor is not causally necessary, or the effect is
observed in only one family. Combining those unrelated successes would not
create a source-identical rule and would violate the one-factor policy.

## Observation, interpretation, confidence and decision

**Observation.** The protocol has often tested a stronger L2/L3 claim than the
minimum wording of the G1 decision rule. Re-evaluating every cross-family
mechanism under L1 yields no survivor. The only replicated rule-level positive
is confined to one physical source and has an unresolved classical
system-identification explanation.

**Interpretation.** Foreign-source gain is not logically mandatory for every
NEXTAI principle, so future search should not reject an independently learned
universal rule merely because its parameters do not transfer. This correction
does not rescue any completed mechanism and does not justify an experiment or
protocol change now.
