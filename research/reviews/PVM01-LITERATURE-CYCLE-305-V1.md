# Literature review — cycle 305, completed count 111

This six-result cadence review reuses the primary sources already checked in
the append-only source ledger. It introduces no scientific fit, seed, EXP,
promotion or new architectural commitment. The completed count includes the
pre-fit implementation failure EXP-20261004-0004; failures are not removed from
cadence or budget accounting.

The Transformer reference is grounded in [Vaswani et al. 2017, SRC-0436/0441](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762).
Dense self/cross attention and feed-forward blocks identify the conventional
family. They do not guarantee competence for NEXTAI's numeric paired-view task;
that competence must be observed under the preregistered gates. Independent
query masking preserves the declared single-query inference semantics.

[Matching Networks, SRC-0439](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.04080) establishes learned
embeddings followed by support matching as prior art. A successful PVM01 learned
alignment plus exact nearest-neighbor control would be a useful local mechanism
diagnosis, not discovery of a new retrieval architecture. Its complete transport,
support ingestion, updates, scan, threshold and decoding costs must be charged.

[Williams and Seeger 2000, SRC-0442](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2000/hash/19de10adbaa1b2ee13f77f679fa1483a-Abstract.html)
is the primary reference for the Nyström approximation. The prospective kernel
transport uses landmark whitening and train-only selection; ridge transport is
also a mandatory classical alternative. The task demands evidence that training
helps; it does not assume that only neural training can exploit the observations.
Strong classical results are allowed and may close a neural economic proposal.

[Linear Transformers Are Secretly Fast Weight Programmers, SRC-0435](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.11174)
and [Gated Delta Networks, SRC-0440](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.06464) are direct
prior art for fast-weight and delta updates. A future update prototype requires
source-identical causal ablations and full-cost comparisons, not novelty credit
for a delta rule. No such prototype was fitted or evaluated in cycle 305.

[Zoology, SRC-0438](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.04927) motivates associative recall
as a diagnostic proxy. Its findings do not establish language transfer for
NEXTAI or turn update-round labels into reasoning depth. Random independent
values and growing K preserve a meaningful memory-capacity question, but useful
quality must be matched before interpreting costs.

Inference for NEXTAI: first obtain an actual valid paired-view reference result,
separate alignment from ranking and abstention, and preserve exact/classical
retrieval alternatives. EXP-0004 supplies no scientific evidence for choosing
among them. MUC remains a technical control because the public symbolic key
already suffices. The program's economic guards, fresh-final requirement and
unexecuted mechanism/ablation/adverse scope remain unchanged.
