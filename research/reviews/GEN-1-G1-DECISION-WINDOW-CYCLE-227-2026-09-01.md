## Causal intervention map

The last twelve results are not twelve independent attempts at an LLM
successor. They instantiate five recurring interventions:

1. **Learned state/update versus bounded or frozen state.** EXP-0042 learned a
   pushdown transition and replicated forward closure-chain depth transfer;
   EXP-0044 failed every target under the recoverable inverse operation. The
   learned signature is narrow forward stack execution, not reusable operator
   transfer.
2. **Learned search guidance versus frozen exact structure.** EXP-0045 and
   EXP-0059 preserved exact answers and reduced enumeration, but frozen
   incumbent/pattern guidance won after fit, certification, state and repeated
   workload. The exact incumbent, pair-pattern selector, certificate, branch
   order, cache and reuse variants are closed.
3. **Learned local selector/update versus shuffled, frozen or dense credit.**
   EXP-0048, EXP-0049 and EXP-0051 produced local updates or near-zero query-K
   slopes, but useful quality gains were absent or reversed. Eligibility,
   error/novelty trees and predictive-coordinate variants are closed.
4. **Learned addressing versus raw/frozen/classical indexing.** EXP-0054 and
   EXP-0056 retained local insertion but lost quality and full-cost scaling to
   frozen encoders, raw nearest-neighbour and exact k-d trees. Flat hashes,
   pair-trained hierarchical routing and posterior-partition DAG aliases are
   closed.
5. **Shared sparse representation versus independent/dense/classical
   structure.** EXP-0057 had near-zero cross-family quality while contextual
   Chow--Liu remained the high-quality Pareto point. Sparse set-memory slot,
   width, top-k and router variants are closed.

The preserved learned signature is the three-seed, disjoint-corpus forward

## Canonical eight-result gate

The append-only window ID is `G1-POST-EXP-0059-V1`, starting after
EXP-20260901-0059 with qualifying count zero and maximum eight. A qualifying
item is a scientifically valid scored experiment on a genuinely different
mechanism. Service cycles, invalid plans, pre-seed invalidations and aliases do
not count.

One exact mechanism receives at most one quick; only a strong positive permits
one unchanged multi-seed screen and one preregistered adversarial variant. A
negative closes it without tuning. Every mechanism must prospectively explain
the prior failure it repairs, why frozen/classical controls cannot produce the
effect, the qualitative signature beyond marginal accuracy, and the expected
full-cost scaling. Existing cohorts are mandatory when sufficient; a new
cohort needs a prior service-only missing-discriminator argument. At most two
consecutive cycles may omit scoring. HYP-0012 remains an accounting control and
does not gain confidence automatically from unrelated failures.

After eight qualifying experiments, a no-scoring phase review may continue G1
only if at least one observation-learned source-identical mechanism shows a
causal gain at matched useful quality, works in two frozen families/tasks and
on an unseen operation, survives three seeds and a preregistered adversarial
variant, retains a local-update or other declared qualitative signature, is
implementably Pareto-nondominated at full end-to-end cost, and uses no manual
ontology, privileged support or hidden preprocessing. Otherwise architecture
scoring stops and the user is asked for a strategic reset.
