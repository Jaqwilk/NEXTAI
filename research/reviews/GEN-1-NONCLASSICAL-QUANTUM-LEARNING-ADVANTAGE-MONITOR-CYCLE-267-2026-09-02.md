## Audited observations

### Genuine quantum-native sample advantage

`SRC-0321` is the strongest anomaly. A scalable squeezed-light platform uses
EPR-entangled probe-memory pairs and Bell measurements to learn multimode
random-displacement processes. In the paper's randomized hypothesis-testing
game, a 120-mode experiment with `100000` samples reaches success probability
`0.563 ± 0.025`; the reported entanglement-free bound is only
`0.5 + 3.8e-11`, corresponding to a `9.2`-order sample advantage. The result
varies squeezing and spans several mode counts, so it is not merely a
single-point hardware anecdote.

The distinction in the paper matters. Its headline `11.8`-order reconstruction
improvement compares `~1e7` samples at 100 modes with an extrapolated vacuum
requirement of `7.3e18`. The authors explicitly state that this reconstruction
experiment alone is not a proof because the classical lower bound covers a
larger process family and parameter range. Their randomized game repairs that
logical gap and does provide a genuine quantum-native information edge.

It still does not supply the NEXTAI mechanism. The EPR source, external quantum
memory and Bell measurement are a fixed authored protocol; the executable
update rule is not learned from ordinary observations. The target is one family
of quantum displacement channels rather than unrelated task families, and the
reported boundary omits a conservative cost for state generation, optics,
control, calibration and energy. Simulating this protocol locally would remove
the causal physical resource rather than test it.

### Superconducting continual learning

`SRC-0322` sequentially trains an 18-transmon variational classifier on
Fashion-MNIST, MRI and quantum-phase classification and retains mean accuracy
above `92.3%` using elastic weight consolidation. This is multi-task physical
hardware evidence, but the retention rule is established classical EWC. The
classical image inputs are PCA-compressed to ten dimensions, and the comparison
showing a quantum advantage uses a task whose labels are generated by a random
quantum circuit. A small feed-forward network is not a sufficient substitute
for matched kernels, tensor-network simulators and other strong classical
feature controls. The study also lacks a matched full system-cost boundary.

### Correlated-spin reservoir

`SRC-0323` reports a nine-spin physical quantum reservoir with one-to-two orders
lower temporal prediction error than earlier quantum reservoirs and better
long-horizon weather forecasting than classical reservoirs with thousands of
nodes. Its natural many-body dynamics are deliberately fixed; only a classical
linear readout is trained. It is therefore a hardware feature-map claim, not a
learned nonclassical update rule. Without a factor-specific matched surrogate
and full acquisition/control cost, node count does not establish efficiency.

### Classical-simulability falsifier

`SRC-0324` supplies the strongest control missing from generic quantum-learning
claims. A classical surrogate based on low-bodyness Pauli propagation, tensor
networks and classical shadows matches or outperforms standard QCNNs on all
tested datasets up to 1024 qubits. The analysis attributes benchmark success to
locally easy datasets and low-bodyness observables. Thus qubit count, circuit
depth or a quantum device label cannot by themselves identify a nonclassical
causal edge in the regime actually used for learning.

## Observation

Nonclassical physics can create a real information edge when the source itself
is quantum and the learner may retain an entangled quantum memory. The
photonic result adds that edge to the cycle-261 causal boundary. It does not,
however, identify a lifetime-learned, portable computational rule: its gain is
carried by a fixed source-matched probe and measurement interface. Claims on
ordinary classical datasets instead rely on classical learning rules,
quantum-generated labels, manual compression, fixed reservoirs or classical
regimes for which strong surrogates erase the apparent advantage.

## Interpretation, uncertainty and confidence

The correct conclusion is not that quantum learning has no advantage. It is
that the demonstrated advantage belongs to a specialized physical information
channel, not yet to the observation-learned source-identical mechanism sought
by NEXTAI. A software benchmark cannot honestly test that channel, and building
one would only encode the desired quantum feature map into frozen apparatus.

Confidence is `0.995` that none of `SRC-0321` through `SRC-0324` passes the
prospective gate, `0.99` that the photonic work establishes a genuine
quantum-native sample advantage but not a learned cross-family rule, `0.97`
that the superconducting continual-learning comparison is confounded by EWC,
PCA and quantum-generated labels, and `0.98` that the correlated-spin proposal
is a fixed reservoir. Confidence is only `0.65` that no future physical
experiment can combine a learned portable rule with nonclassical advantage and
conservative full cost.
