## Audited observations

### Non-equilibrium memory has a positive dissipation cost

`SRC-0325` studies Temporal Contrastive Learning, where integral feedback
creates an implicit memory of successive free and clamped states. Its
microscopically reversible Markov model gives the clearest thermodynamic
discriminator found in this cycle. At detailed balance, dissipation is zero,
but the memory kernel is monotonic and cannot approximate the temporal
difference needed by the learning rule. Driving the model further from
equilibrium produces the required positive and negative kernel lobes, lowers
update error and increases dissipation.

This is a model and MNIST simulation rather than an end-to-end physical
learner. Nevertheless, its causal result contradicts the attractive shortcut:
reversibility does not make learning memory free. The comparison needed for a
weight update requires temporary storage and erasure, and better approximation
of that comparison pays a Landauer-like dissipation cost. The contrastive rule,
clamping schedule and target labels are also authored rather than learned.

### Power-efficient self-learning resistor circuits

`SRC-0326` is the strongest physical adaptive-energy anomaly. Variable-resistor
networks use local coupled learning; the authors add a power-minimizing term,
choose low-conductance initialization and dynamically weight error versus power.
Laboratory experiments show lower trained free power, and the paper estimates
training energy by integrating free power over training time.

The result is an explicit error-power tradeoff: stronger power reduction can
decrease accuracy. The desired output, coupled-learning update and power
penalty are all supplied, so the device does not discover its learning rule.
The main experimental setting is one resistor substrate and regression-style
task, while mechanical transfer is simulated. Its training-energy boundary
does not include the complete supervisor, twin-state comparison, global clock,
measurement and resistance-update electronics. This is useful physical
regularization, not a source-identical matched-quality energy edge.

### Task-adaptive magnetic reservoir

`SRC-0327` uses one chiral magnet in skyrmion, conical and hybrid phases for
chaotic forecasting, nonlinear waveform transformation and combined tasks.
Different phases expose different memory/nonlinearity tradeoffs, and the same
material can be reconfigured by field and temperature. However, the phase and
input encoding are chosen for each task, the reservoir dynamics are fixed, and
each target trains its own ridge readout. The main measurements use microwave
spectroscopy including operation near `4 K`; no matched end-to-end energy is
reported. This is external model selection over fixed physical features.

### Stochastic processing unit

`SRC-0328` physically realizes three reproducible eight-cell RLC thermodynamic
computers for Gaussian sampling and `8 x 8` matrix inversion. It is genuine
hardware computation, but the user maps a supplied covariance or precision
matrix directly into switched capacitances. No state-update rule is learned.
The large-scale speed and energy advantage is a mathematical projection using
assumed 16-bit components, `1 microsecond` physical dynamics and `0.005 mW` per
cell; it is not a measured scaled-system crossover.

## Observation

Thermodynamic hardware can execute a fixed computation efficiently, and a
physical learner can trade accuracy for lower inference power under an authored
energy objective. Neither fact creates new task-relevant information or teaches
the update rule. The only audited source isolating reversibility during learning
finds the opposite of a free-computation signature: accurate temporal credit
requires non-equilibrium memory and positive dissipation.

No source adds an observation-learned edge to the cycle-261 causal boundary.
The apparent gains reduce to a fixed physical feature map, a classical local
learning rule, explicit power regularization, external task selection or a
projected device-level cost.

## Interpretation, uncertainty and confidence

but learning which distinctions to retain still requires observations, a
criterion and irreversible state change. A local software implementation would
collapse to already tested pruning, compression, sparse updates or MDL; a
physical claim would require hardware measurements unavailable to the local
harness. Creating a benchmark now would encode the desired accounting result
rather than test a new causal thesis.
