## Deduplication result

The proposed source-identical tractable sum-product-circuit successor is not a
new mechanism. `EXP-20260830-0040` already compared a learned decomposable SPN
with contextual Chow--Liu. Both produced identical cold/near/retention,
probability errors, query work, state, circuit size and updates, while the
learned label paid more fit and R16 work and was absent from the implementable
Pareto frontier. HYP-0013 has remained dormant since that registered null.

Cycle 175 then tested the exact proposed escape hatch: a generic recursive
context circuit. It rejected the benchmark before migration because finite
observational distributions admit equivalent flat sums of products, decision
diagrams, factor graphs and variable-elimination executions. Recursive node
count depends on variable order, sharing and compiler conventions. A generator
authored as a recursive circuit would privilege the desired representation,
and the named existing controls did not implement the required generic
algorithms.

## Why the four-family v5 evaluator does not repair identifiability

The frozen lossless evaluator is valuable for shared transfer, but only one of
its four worlds has native probabilistic-circuit semantics. The other three are
predictive-state, local-dynamics and behavioral-program mappings serialized as
anonymous public tokens. A common circuit would therefore need a generic
output-binding or decoder rule. `EXP-20260901-0057` already showed that this
binding dominated learned sparse-memory behavior while contextual Chow--Liu
was the sole useful Pareto point. A score could not attribute a gain or failure
to factorization rather than the decoder.

Test worlds provide public support objects but no labeled held-out query-target
pairs during fit. Consequently, a proposed support-only learned-structure role
would not be a matched supervised factorization ablation. Adding labeled test
support, recursive generator metadata, variable-order conventions or a circuit
oracle would change the data boundary and create precisely the target-shaped
microbenchmark the program forbids.

## Decision

Reject `cross_family_tractable_sum_product_circuit_v6` before protected
migration. Do not add role names, schema fields, candidate wrappers or a renamed
HYP-0013 event. Preserve the proposal as a documented duplicate rather than
spending one of the eight G1 experiments on an alias. This is an identifiability
and portfolio decision, not new evidence against all probabilistic circuits.
