## Observation

DS08a, DronePropA and continuous-event prediction all admit a mechanical continuous-dynamics view. One frozen rule casts native finite numeric matrices to float32, copies them into the upper-left of fixed tensors and publishes ordinary occupancy masks. It uses no family label, path, class name, semantic channel name, projection, manual alignment or target-informed transform.

The real-file/development smoke produced identical common shapes for all families: support input and target `108×32`, history `32×32`, future public tensor and output `50×32`. Native DS08a shapes are `18→14`, DronePropA `10→6`, and continuous event `32→1`. All copies are finite and exactly recoverable after the declared float32 cast; DS08a remains inside one unit/cycle and DronePropA inside one whole flight.

The four causal assignments are constructible without changing learner source: pooled shared slow fit; three independent family clones with summed cost; leave-one-family-out `cross_family_only`; and empty-slow-fit `support_only`. A common masked normalized MSE can average within world and equally across families while retaining every raw family metric.

V1 incorrectly named the leave-one-family-out control `no_cross_family`. It is preserved immutable and marked invalid before protected implementation. V2 changes only the name to `cross_family_only` and the corresponding success statement; no predictive result existed.

## Interpretation and uncertainty

This interface does not hide family identity—the masks and distributions make it observable. That is reported as a confound, never as transfer. The causal signature is stricter: shared must beat independent, and cross-family-only slow fit must beat support-only in every family. An implicit specialist that shares nothing cannot satisfy both comparisons merely by routing.

The common tensors are mechanically valid, but a future shared learner may still be scientifically vacuous or dominated by native specialists. Confidence is `0.93` in the shape, loss and ablation construction, and deliberately low in any positive outcome before scoring.
