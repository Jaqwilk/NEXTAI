## Audited near-anomalies

### Shared neural schemas in primates

`SRC-0301` reports faster acquisition and stable decision-related neural
subspaces across sequential visuomotor mappings in three macaques. `SRC-0302`
finds colour, shape and motor subspaces reused compositionally across three
tasks in two macaques. These are valuable empirical contradictions to a claim
that biological reuse never occurs.

They do not expose one causal update rule. Both experiments use researcher-
defined visual categories, button or saccade actions, correctness feedback and
juice reward. The tasks share one experimental ontology rather than spanning
unrelated natural families. Neural subspaces are decoded after observation;
neither study removes and rescues the update factor, and neither measures a
learning-to-reuse energy/state crossover.

### Loopy cellular plasticity

`SRC-0303` is the strongest embodied near-candidate. A 36-cell physical robot
changes stiffness and damping using a reaction-diffusion cellular-plasticity
model and can outperform empirically optimized static settings under
confinement. The response is local in the model and happens during operation.

The experiment remains one platform. A central computer simulates all cells;
an external script changes phase parameters; the authors define stiffness and
damping factories, position-error and velocity consumption signals, asymmetric
opposition constants and morphology transitions. These engineered mappings are
the causal interface. Static controls are informative, but there is no
unrelated second substrate, learned-rule ablation or full sensing-compute-
actuation-state-energy accounting.

### Thermal neoHebbian synapse

`SRC-0304` experimentally validates a ReRAM/heater device that stores a weight
and a thermal eligibility trace. Its system utility is then simulated for
phoneme classification and path-planning reinforcement learning. The paper
uses e-prop in one task and a modified rule in the other; labels and reward
provide credit. Component physics is real, but cross-domain source identity,
system-level intervention and full measured cost are absent.

### Compiled workflow model

`SRC-0305` reports substantial deployment savings after distilling authored
travel, support and insurance workflows into separately fine-tuned small
models, including a projected break-even after repeated conversations. This is
the strongest explicit amortization near-anomaly.

The useful information comes from domain flowcharts, synthetic scenarios
generated with frontier models and external model judges. The fitted artifacts
are domain-specific dense models, not one source-identical observation-learned
rule. Price-based inference savings and a one-time fine-tuning estimate do not
create a new information edge, and the workflow ontology carries the target
procedure. It also depends on external models, which cannot become NEXTAI
candidate machinery.

### Physical-network landscape coupling

`SRC-0306` derives a generic relation between physical and task-cost Hessians
for linear-response networks, illustrated with adaptable electrical resistors.
It explicitly begins from a desired-output cost and supplies theory rather than
a cross-substrate empirical learned-factor intervention. It therefore confirms
the cycle-261 prior/credit edge instead of violating it.

## Observation

Six newly audited near-anomalies span biological reuse, embodied adaptation,
neuromorphic hardware, physical learning theory and compilation amortization.
Every one fails at least the source-identity and ontology/credit gates. None
identifies an information-bearing edge absent from the cycle-261 DAG, and none
overturns the recorded fate of an existing edge with the required causal and
full-cost evidence.

## Interpretation and confidence

The monitoring result is negative but informative. Modern empirical work does
show reuse, local adaptation and amortization separately. The conjunction fails
because task semantics, reward, a domain workflow, engineered sensor-effector
mapping or fixed substrate dynamics supplies the useful alignment, while cost
is reported for only part of the system.

- Confidence `0.99` that none of the six sources meets the frozen trigger.
- Confidence `0.98` that the primate studies demonstrate shared representation
  rather than a factor-specific common update rule.
- Confidence `0.99` that Loopy is a single engineered platform with centralized
  simulation and authored phenotype/stimulus mappings.
- Confidence `0.98` that the neoHebbian task demonstrations are not one
  unchanged empirical system rule across domains.
- Confidence `0.97` that workflow compilation moves authored procedure and
  frontier-model work into training rather than exposing a new information
  source.
