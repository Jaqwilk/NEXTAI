# GEN-1 — identifiability and portfolio review, cycle 128

## Scope

This was exactly one no-scoring portfolio and primary-literature review after
EXP-20260831-0009. It created no hypothesis, experiment plan, scoring seed,
candidate, benchmark, schema or protected evaluator change. It did not run the
audited scoring harness and did not change any scientific confidence. The active
cohort remains `heldout_three_family_continuous_transfer_v7`.

The review asks whether a genuinely different principle is ready for an
immutable experiment on an existing frozen cohort. It does not treat an external
paper's score as NEXTAI evidence.

## 1. What was objectively learned?

The recent causal contrasts separate within-source regularization from transfer.
EXP-0006 and its three-seed EXP-0007 replication showed that a compact recurrent
residual with slot-local updates can improve one physical WT changepoint task.
The source-identical rule then failed every heterogeneous transfer cell in
EXP-0008. EXP-0009 repeated the separation at a lower level: the shared six-rate
update law beat its independently fitted ablation, but foreign-family-only
training lost to support-only in all nine cells and was unstable. Its aggregate
NRMSE advantage over persistence was only `0.0065182916`, below the frozen
`0.0380685515` meaningful-effect threshold and at higher full workload.

Across EXP-0057 through EXP-0009, shared low-rank dynamics, masked spectra,
bounded residuals, expert mixtures, predictive indices and learned update rates
all failed their foreign-family causal contrast. The common signal is that
pooling can regularize a model without identifying a representation or operator
that transfers between anonymous, semantically unrelated coordinate systems.

No current learned candidate is replicated, cross-family positive and
implementably Pareto non-dominated. HYP-0012 therefore remains the leading
portfolio-level account; this review adds no scientific evidence to it.

## 2. Which assumptions were falsified?

- Exchangeability or permutation equivariance alone does not create a common
  semantic coordinate system.
- Sharing fewer parameters is not sufficient: transferring only a six-scalar
  update table still failed the foreign-family test.
- A shared-versus-independent gain is not transfer evidence when pooled source
  coverage or regularization can explain it. The foreign-only versus support-only
  contrast is necessary.
- Local updates and bounded state can be useful within one system without being
  portable across unrelated systems.
- Renaming a recurrence as fast memory, test-time training or learned
  optimization would not create a new causal principle unless an experiment
  isolates behavior beyond the existing LMS/RLS, recurrent and support controls.

## 3. Which results replicated?

Only the WT bounded-recurrent-residual screening signal replicated across three
runner-random seeds and relabelings in EXP-0007. It did not replicate across
families in EXP-0008. No heterogeneous transfer mechanism has a positive
multi-seed replication. Classical persistence, adaptive linear estimation,
probabilistic structure and exact finite algorithms repeatedly remain strong
full-cost controls.

## 4. Is the portfolio trapped in one family?

The benchmark inventory is broad—finite programs, graphs, compression,
parallel infilling, physical changepoints and heterogeneous continuous worlds—
but recent candidate selection is trapped around one abstract move: infer an
anonymous shared coordinate, parameter, key, residual, expert weight or update
rule. Another parameterization of that move has low expected information gain.

The next useful distinction is not neural versus symbolic. It is identifiable
versus unidentifiable observation structure. A new representation experiment is
admissible only if the data provide a mechanically observable symmetry breaker
that is neither a family label nor a human ontology.

## 5. Are implementation details being optimized instead of principles?

Yes, if work continues by changing rank, bins, learning rates, residual bounds,
hashes, routing temperatures or memory depth after the valid negative results.
Those changes would tune implementations of dormant or falsified theses. This
review authorizes none of them.

## 6. What result would most change current beliefs?

The highest-value result would be a preregistered demonstration that one
source-identical learner uses an observable auxiliary structure to recover the
same latent factor or operator across at least three anonymous worlds, while:

1. an auxiliary-randomization control destroys the effect;
2. source-identical independent and support-only ablations do not obtain it;
3. the effect holds in every family and at three frozen scales;
4. classical PCA/ICA/CCA, adaptive filters, Chow–Liu, autoregressive and exact
   applicable controls are matched on quality and fully charged cost;
5. query/update work is local and does not scan dormant knowledge.

Without the randomized-auxiliary causal contrast, another positive pooled score
would not change the central belief.

## 7. Which prior work already contains the apparent novelty?

Locatello et al. (SRC-0164) prove that unsupervised disentanglement is not
identifiable without assumptions on models and data. Hyvarinen et al.
(SRC-0166) obtain nonlinear-ICA identifiability by conditioning on an observable
auxiliary variable. Locatello et al. (SRC-0165) show that non-i.i.d. pairs sharing
latent factors can supply weak supervision without naming the factors. Ahuja et
al. (SRC-0167) obtain causal representation guarantees from interventions only
under explicit support and intervention assumptions. Morioka and Hyvarinen
(SRC-0168) instead assume suitable observable-variable grouping. These are not
interchangeable guarantees; NEXTAI must verify the selected assumption rather
than cite the paper and hope it applies.

Fast-weight and test-time-memory proposals are not a clean escape. Schlag et al.
(SRC-0169) show linear attention is fast-weight programming and introduce a
delta-rule update. TTT-Linear (SRC-0170) contains an exact linear-attention
equivalence. Titans (SRC-0171) adds surprise, momentum, forgetting, persistent
parameters and sometimes sliding-window attention. A small local version would
confound update law, recurrence, routing and memory capacity already tested;
reproducing the scaled system is outside the current bounded local cohorts.

## 8. Three candidate directions and decision

### A. Auxiliary-structured causal representation — retain only for a gate

This is the only direction with a potentially new causal factor: the learner
would receive a mechanically observable pairing or auxiliary variable whose
randomization can be ablated. It is not yet a hypothesis. The frozen three-family
cohort has anonymous sequences and public support, but it has not been shown that
one source-identical auxiliary relation satisfies any cited identifiability
condition in all three families. Treating native controls, intervention labels,
world IDs or channel groups as equivalent would recreate a manual ontology.

### B. Fast-weight or TTT memory — reject without scoring

At bounded local scale this reduces to a recurrent matrix state updated by an
outer-product, delta rule or self-supervised gradient. HYP-0014, HYP-0028 and
HYP-0029 already isolate the relevant update/recurrent contrasts against LMS,
RLS and persistence. A larger inner MLP would change capacity and cost together
without isolating a new principle.

### C. Surprise-gated deep memory — reject for current cohorts

Surprise, momentum and forgetting could be useful engineering choices, but an
honest Titans-like test needs a scaled sequence model and matched modern neural
controls. A tiny version on WT or repository bytes would be a post-result gate,
replay or update-law variant; current data cannot separate the claimed long-term
memory principle from those simpler explanations.

Decision: `select_none_before_identifiability_gate`. Do not create HYP-0030, a
plan or a new cohort in this wake. The absence of a justified experiment is an
informative portfolio result, not permission to lower the gate.

## Exact next discriminating cycle

Perform one no-scoring, development-only `observable_auxiliary_identifiability`
audit over the existing `heldout_three_family_continuous_transfer_v7` and
`heldout_wt_changepoints_prequential_v1` contracts. Do not read held-out targets,
change a benchmark or implement a candidate.

The audit must enumerate every mechanically candidate-visible pairing,
nonstationarity, control and grouping signal, then retain at most one only if:

- the same rule is available in every claimed source and test world without
  family names, paths, native types, semantic channel names or hidden event IDs;
- a cited identifiability theorem's assumptions can be mapped field-by-field to
  the frozen observations rather than inferred from a correlation;
- train/development-only statistics show at least three independent auxiliary
  states and enough variation/rank for the theorem's contrast;
- an auxiliary-randomization or pair-breaking negative control is definable
  without target access and should reduce alignment to chance;
- the existing cohort can charge acquisition, representation fit, local update,
  query, bytes, resident/peak state and R1/R4/R16 work at three scales;
- strong source-identical classical controls can be run without a schema or
  evaluator change.

If any condition fails, append `no_observable_auxiliary_identifiability_contract`
and keep the representation family dormant. If all pass, the following wake may
create one low-confidence hypothesis and preregister one quick; a service
migration is allowed only if the audit proves scientific need and receives the
required protected-change authorization.
