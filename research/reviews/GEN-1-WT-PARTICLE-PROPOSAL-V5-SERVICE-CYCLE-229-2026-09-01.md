and EXP-0048's error-gated eligibility trace produced only a tiny quality gain.

proposal allocation over bounded alternative predictive states handles

or deterministic posterior-mean propagation. Persistence, LMS, RLS, transition
bank, bounded replay, ridge FIR, spectral PSR and CSSR remain mandatory, so
generic online adaptation or a classical state estimator cannot receive novelty
