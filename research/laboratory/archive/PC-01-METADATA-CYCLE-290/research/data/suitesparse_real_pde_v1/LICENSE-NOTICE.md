# SuiteSparse Matrix Collection data notice

The locally downloaded matrix payloads in `archive/` and `extracted/` are
excluded from Git. The matrices are distributed by the SuiteSparse Matrix
Collection under CC-BY 4.0. Preserve every Matrix Market header and embedded
matrix-specific citation without modification.

Collection citations:

- Timothy A. Davis and Yifan Hu, “The University of Florida Sparse Matrix
  Collection,” ACM Transactions on Mathematical Software 38(1), 2011,
  https://doi.org/10.1145/2049662.2049663.
- Scott P. Kolodziej et al., “The SuiteSparse Matrix Collection Website
  Interface,” Journal of Open Source Software 4(35), 2019,
  https://doi.org/10.21105/joss.01244.

Collection and license information: https://sparse.tamu.edu/about.

This snapshot is used only for local NEXTAI research. Any exclusion after
download must be recorded by the prospective algebraic audit; payloads may not
be silently replaced because of candidate performance.
