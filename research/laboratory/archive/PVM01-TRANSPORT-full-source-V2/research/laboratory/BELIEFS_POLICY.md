# Prospective use of the external beliefs draft

Effective LAB-RESTART-20260904-V1. research/BELIEFS.json is preserved unchanged
as the external auditor's 2026-09-03 estimates, not adopted as a reward function.
Its minimum_reportable_shift=0.05 rule is superseded prospectively by this policy.

- Use belief IDs as a question index. Evidence and informative nulls count even
  when no numerical probability changes. Do not manufacture precision.
- Any new belief assessment is a separate dated event/document with evidence,
  alternative explanations, uncertainty and the exact tested scope. Never edit
  the old estimates or increase unrelated beliefs because a candidate failed.
- B01 becomes several controls for learning, metric sensitivity and economic
  measurement. A transformer need not satisfy all legacy G1 claims at once.
- B02 is scenario- and backend-specific. Query operation ranks are not a
  universal predictor of GPU time or energy. Energy remains unmeasured unless
  a valid energy measurement is actually performed.
- B04 must use the historical WT hash. The actual rule includes a ridge VAR(2)
  representation, RLS adaptation and origin-relative clipping. Attribute gains
  only after isolating these factors and evaluating independent traces.
- B10 (integration) is untested, not an established exclusive path to progress.
- B12 must not demand superiority to every possible polynomial sufficient
  statistic. Learned algorithms can themselves be represented that way. Test
  declared competent alternatives at matched quality and full cost instead.
- B15's confidence-shift reward is rejected: a well-controlled null, a repaired
  reproducer or an honest budget blocker can be useful progress without a shift.
- Prior scientific-validity exclusions remain terminal for their experiment IDs.
  Interpretative corrections do not magically turn historical negatives into
  passing results. Reopening a narrower question requires a new preregistration.
