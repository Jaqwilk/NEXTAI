# INTERPRETATION

The exact preregistered principle survives its quick screen. A recursively
rolled transition learned from response increments preserves useful response
dynamics well beyond the 32 fitted target steps, and target-revealed RLS state
updates remain local to each anonymous file. Direct FIR, memory and routing
controls do not reproduce the quality on this realized seed.

This does not establish novelty or an LLM-successor mechanism. DMD with
control, VAR/system identification and adaptive RLS are direct classical
precedents. The experiment supports a narrower claim: on these real physical
changepoints, an explicitly recurrent residual representation plus local
adaptation gives a large capability improvement while remaining Pareto
non-dominated after full charging. The unchanged quality across K also means
there is no positive knowledge-scaling law here; only query/state independence
from dormant K and depth extrapolation are observed.

# ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

- Slot-local RLS adaptation may account for part of the gain independently of
  the recurrent representation. The existing direct-curve RLS control is not
  a source-identical recurrent no-update ablation, so this causal component is
  not isolated by EXP-0006.
- The fixed ±4 bound may act as a favorable stability prior. Changing or
  removing it now would be outcome-informed tuning; the unchanged replication
  must retain it.
- Both test files share the same apparatus and generator. Cross-file success
  is not cross-domain transfer, and H96 is evaluator-created rather than
  naturally longer post-change OOD.
- Classical linear system identification may be the complete explanation.
  A replicated result would be useful engineering/scaling evidence, not a new
  computational principle without a later discriminating ablation and broader
  world transfer.

# DECISION

`replicate`, not `promote`. Preserve EXP-0006 as valid positive screening
evidence. Do not alter ridge `0.001`, features, residual bound, RLS order,
candidate code, cohort, controls or thresholds. The candidate is promising in
the ordinary-language sense but is not assigned machine status `promising`
because it has only one seed and no adversarial variant.
