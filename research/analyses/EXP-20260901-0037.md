# INTERPRETATION

The score-reassignment ablation shows that observation-driven selection found
better programs than chance assignment or persistence inside exactly the same
proposal population. This is a real selection effect, not merely different
candidate code or random coverage. It also preserved the desired sparse local
execution signature.

The frozen language was nevertheless too weak as a predictive representation.
Selecting one independently scored scalar expression per anonymous output
channel did not capture the channel-coupled nonlinear dynamics needed for OOD
rollout. The error compounded until no task crossed the accuracy threshold,
whereas ordinary local ridge retained much lower continuous error, nonzero
accuracy and continual capability. Exhaustively evaluating the fixed population
then made discovery much more expensive than ridge despite cheaper individual
queries.

Adding more terms, vector-valued primitives, fitted coefficients, adaptive
parent proposals, a larger population or another score would change the exact
registered causal mechanism after seeing its failure. Those are not authorized
repairs or replications. The observation supports selection as an active
component but falsifies the claimed capability-per-full-cost conjunction.

# ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

The random proposal population may simply omit a sufficiently accurate short
expression; a different language or adaptive proposal distribution could make
selection useful. That explanation is plausible but changes the frozen tested
factor and cannot rescue this experiment.

Ridge receives continuously fitted coefficients and cross-coordinate linear
features, while the population uses a coarse coefficient grid. This makes
ridge a strong but not source-identical comparator. The shuffled and frozen
roles isolate the selection effect; the ridge comparison answers the separate
registered question of whether that effect is competitive at matched system
boundaries.

One seed might change which random programs exist. It cannot plausibly close
the observed `0.806` aggregate NRMSE gap without being a new seed search, and
the plan permits no replication after failure.

# DECISION

sparse interpreter. Do not replicate, enlarge or tune it. Preserve the
