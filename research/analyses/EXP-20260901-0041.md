# INTERPRETATION

The complete v11 result supports the narrow causal claim that learned full
pushdown state, rather than the source-identical depth-two capacity or frozen
transition map, causes exact closure-chain completion beyond training depth on
this cohort. Finite-order, dense autoregressive, bidirectional/parallel Markov
and Re-Pair controls did not reproduce whole-chain completion, and all
registered kill gates passed.

This is not evidence of universal cost superiority. The main learner remains
quality/cost non-dominated because simpler controls are cheaper but inexact;
it does not dominate them in fit, critical path or repeated-use workload. It
is also an exhaustive learner for a seven-symbol deterministic delimiter
grammar, not a demonstrated general representation learner or LLM successor.

The positive outcome policy is now applicable, but it authorizes only an
unchanged three-seed adversarial new-corpus replication. The two earlier
runner seeds are useful consistency diagnostics, not substitutes for that
registered replication because they reuse the same corpus and were incomplete.

# ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

- The learner may merely identify one closed balanced-delimiter grammar whose
  sufficient structure is unusually clean after token projection.
- All three runner seeds permute and resample the same frozen corpus; corpus
  idiosyncrasy is not averaged away.
- Exact-span quality alone keeps the learner Pareto-nondominated while Re-Pair
  and PPM retain substantially lower fit, critical-path or R16 costs.
- The evaluator supplies only valid balanced traces; malformed records,
  irrelevant events or a different stack alphabet may expose brittleness.
- The privileged control establishes a lower-bound path, not an implementable
  competitor, and is therefore excluded from implementable Pareto evidence.
