# INTERPRETATION

Within this narrow real-source delimiter projection, the exact difference from
the source-identical depth-two role supports the causal claim that retaining an
unbounded-by-training-depth stack, rather than only learning the pair grammar,
enables D=3/4/5 closure-chain completion. The frozen role separately shows that
the runner-random symbol pairing was learned from balanced observations rather
than supplied byte identities. Failure of PPM, CTW, bidirectional Markov,
parallel BP and Re-Pair to complete whole chains suggests the signature is not
explained by local sequence statistics on this cohort.

This does not establish a general reusable reasoning mechanism. The state
update is a classical learned pushdown parser over seven delimiter symbols, the
corpus is locally visible, and the query critical path scans the trace
sequentially. Its `3.77M` fit-operation search is also materially more expensive
than the strongest grammar/context controls. The valid evidence is therefore a
specific OOD-depth signature, not an LLM-successor or broad scaling claim.

The privileged-control crash is an infrastructure incompatibility, not a
candidate failure. Because the preregistration required every mandatory control
to complete, the run cannot satisfy its positive outcome policy even though all
implementable comparisons and causal ablations completed decisively.

# CONFIDENCE

Confidence is `0.85` that the exact v9 implementation uses full stack capacity

# ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

- The benchmark may reward a specialized balanced-delimiter solver rather than
  a generally reusable learned state transition.
- Exact point-mass predictions make the loss contrast unusually sharp; a noisy
  or ambiguous grammar could remove the advantage.
- First-occurrence-equivariant exhaustive search may exploit the seven-symbol
  closed-world contract and fail when irrelevant symbols or malformed traces
  are present.
- The non-dominated position is quality-driven: Re-Pair and PPM are cheaper on
  major fit/critical-path axes, so a matched-quality implementation could erase
  the apparent efficiency tradeoff.
- The privileged control might expose a separate evaluator issue after its
  current noncontiguous-target indexing bug is repaired.
