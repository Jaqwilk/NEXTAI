## INTERPRETATION

The experiment confirms that raw samples contain enough information to recover the hidden context-specific factorization and compile accurate conditional queries. It does not support a distinct learned probabilistic-circuit advantage. The implemented learned route collapses to the same selector search and conditional pair decomposition as contextual Chow–Liu, then adds bookkeeping work. This is the preregistered classical null.

The large difference between cheap query evaluation and K=`32` structure-discovery work also reinforces the project's recurring full-system lesson: tractable compiled inference can be useful after representation acquisition, while acquisition dominates amortized cost. A compact circuit is not by itself evidence for a better end-to-end learning architecture.

## ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

- The candidate called learned SPN uses the same conditional-matching structure search as the strongest classical control. A genuinely generic recursive independence/instance-split learner could behave differently, so this run rejects the implementation and distinct mechanism claim, not all probabilistic circuits.
- Complete joint samples, one uniquely identifiable binary selector and equal pair noise make structure discovery unusually favorable. Missing data, approximate decompositions or multiple selectors could reverse both accuracy and cost rankings.
- The ordinary Chow–Liu timeout comes from this implementation's inference path and cannot be interpreted as a failure of the Chow–Liu algorithmic family.
- The probability tolerance converts small continuous errors into binary cell failures. MAE and calibration remain strong even where near accuracy is `0.875`, but the threshold was preregistered and cannot be relaxed after seeing the result.
- Fit operation counts are explicit model-operation estimates, not hardware time or a proof of complexity. The two-point K exponent is screening-only.

## DECISION

`discard` the current `learned_decomposable_spn` route and make HYP-0013 `dormant`. The preregistered null and kill conditions both hold: contextual Chow–Liu has identical capability with lower fit/R16 work, the learned candidate is absent from the implementable Pareto frontier, and one near cell is below `0.95`. Preserve contextual probabilistic compilation as a classical mechanism and preserve the Chow–Liu timeout as an implementation failure. Quick cannot promote, and no screen or tuning of this cohort is justified.

Reduce HYP-0013 confidence from `0.30` to `0.24`: sample-learned tractable compilation worked in its best-case world, but no distinct learned advantage survived matched full-system accounting.
