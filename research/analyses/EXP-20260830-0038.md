## INTERPRETATION

Behavioral conjugacy is enough to transfer a reusable program fragment across both opaque token names and independently permuted state representations. The large search reduction is real within this evaluator and is not explained by string identity, unary operation signatures or memoization.

The result does not support a distinct learned architecture. Six primitives have unique pairwise-composition fingerprints, so exact relational canonicalization solves the alignment deterministically. The learned candidate implements the same sufficient invariant while adding fit work and state. The preregistered null is therefore met: cross-representation library transfer survives as a useful classical mechanism, but HYP-0002 has not earned an adversarial screen.

The oracle gap also remains enormous. Even relational reuse performs search whose depth cost grows steeply; discovering one exact macro in a closed six-operation algebra is far from open program induction or a successor to language models.

## ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

- The evaluator supplies complete transition tables. Those tables effectively expose a small semantic ontology; realistic systems may need to acquire comparable invariants from sparse or noisy interaction at much higher cost.
- All six primitives have unique relational fingerprints. Transfer may disappear under automorphisms, partial observability or approximate equivalence where correspondence is non-identifiable.
- The reusable bigram is guaranteed to recur in training. The test isolates alignment and reuse, not whether a system can decide which abstractions are worth inventing in an open library.
- Primitive and relational searches use the same compact enumerator. A stronger e-graph, anti-unification or task-specific dynamic-programming control might narrow or enlarge the constant-factor gap.
- Random's 10/48 cold successes exceed the intuitive per-program chance but have wide binomial uncertainty and zero retention; they do not indicate leakage by themselves.
- The recovery reconstructed audit/execution envelopes after schema failure. Trial payloads came directly from isolated workers, but exact wall seconds and peak RSS were not recoverable and must not be inferred.
