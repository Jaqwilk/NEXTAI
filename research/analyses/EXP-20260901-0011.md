code, as shown by the improvement over frozen random weights. Iterative reveal
also helped slightly over the source-identical one-pass role. The effect is too
small to behave as an error-correcting representation: it never completed an
entire masked span correctly and remained close to uniform coding while a
simple unigram was far stronger and cheaper. Sparse prototype activation is
therefore not a competitive mechanism in this exact frozen configuration.

Formal frontier membership reflects trade-offs across many axes and is not a
substitute for the preregistered causal margins. The negative decision follows
from the paired source-identical contrasts, not from comparison with the
privileged oracle.
