## INTERPRETATION

The evaluator successfully made the question harder and more informative: one source-identical model was fit once on pooled training worlds and tested on four anonymous runner-random worlds, while strong native specialists and oracles verified that every family remained solvable. The result is not a superficial average failure caused by one impossible world; all native specialists were exact outside the probabilistic family.

The current shared mechanism learned no useful transferable representation. Pooling slightly hurt rather than helped the same architecture, and even a generic nearest empirical control was ten times more accurate. The likely proximate cause is not only limited capacity: the numeric ridge head violates the intended permutation-equivariant output commitment. It cannot copy opaque target atoms under unseen relabelings, making zero program transfer unsurprising. Hash-based public serialization may also discard or distort family-specific sufficient statistics.

Therefore EXP-0041 decisively rejects `shared_cross_family_learner` as implemented and shows that naive pooled SVD/ridge learning is not a viable successor principle. It does not cleanly falsify the broader HYP-0015 statement because the candidate failed to instantiate its intended equivariant structured-output mechanism. The strong specialist result is nevertheless meaningful evidence that separate learned/classical sufficient representations currently dominate this generic route.

## ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

- The fixed-width serializer deterministically subsamples long supports. Although acquisition before compression is charged equally, a critical sufficient statistic may be discarded for the shared models while specialists receive native public objects.
- Four meta examples per training world and one fixed training seed may be insufficient to learn a cross-family projection. Increasing examples after seeing this result would be tuning unless preregistered as a new data-scaling question with the same specialist acquisition budget.
- The families have genuinely different output symmetries: probabilities, small discrete trajectories, graph states and opaque program tokens. A universal pointer/probability head may be required; a scalar ridge head is not a neutral implementation of that requirement.
- The contextual specialist's `43/48` probabilistic result could reflect finite-sample parameter error and the `0.05` tolerance rather than structural failure. The preregistered `0.90` family gate still cannot be rounded upward.
- Query-level Wilson intervals ignore shared-world dependence. A replicated screen would be needed for world uncertainty, but no shared candidate earned one.

## DECISION

`discard` the current pooled SVD/ridge implementation. Keep HYP-0015 `uncertain`, not promising or promoted, and reduce confidence from `0.22` to `0.18`. Do not increase meta examples, feature width, SVD rank or ridge parameters in this cohort. Quick has one seed and cannot promote anything.

The registered null holds for the implemented route: transfer and minimum-family gates fail by enormous margins; the pooled learner does not beat its independent ablation; and it is absent from the implementable Pareto front. Because the structured-output equivariance commitment was not faithfully instantiated, the result is an implementation rejection plus adverse family-level evidence, not a general falsification of all shared learners.
