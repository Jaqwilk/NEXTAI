## INTERPRETATION

The tested shared learned update law did not transfer. Pooled meta-selection produced a rule worse than selecting update parameters independently, and both were decisively worse than a fixed normalized LMS rule while costing orders of magnitude more. Recurring-model gating did not preserve useful regimes under the distractor phases.

The experiment also exposed a benchmark-level difficulty boundary. Even realizable classical estimators failed at the short prequential horizons, especially at K=32, while the privileged segmented oracle remained exact. Thus the result cleanly rejects the current learned implementation and the claim that its pooled update provides an advantage; it is weaker evidence about all possible online meta-learning because public histories may be insufficient for reliable regime/function estimation at the quick budget.

The preregistered null nevertheless applies without qualification: shared failed every absolute gate, lost all three causal comparisons to its independent ablation, and was strongly capability/cost dominated by fixed LMS. Increasing phase length, grid width, feature design, thresholds or model-bank size after this result would be unregistered tuning.

- Confidence in HYP-0014 is reduced from `0.28` to `0.08`; status becomes `dormant`, not universally falsified.

## ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

- The fixed quadratic feature expansion grows approximately as K squared and may dilute online learning at K=32. Replacing it after seeing the K split would change the implementation and require a new thesis rather than repair this cohort.
- The meta-objective selected only a learning rate and surprise threshold. A richer learned optimizer might behave differently, but would also add slow-state, meta-fit and update cost and is not supported by this quick.
- Regime changes are statistically invisible before a revealed error. At least the first post-switch prediction is information-theoretically unavoidable, and short phases leave little time to identify a new random function.
- Fixed LMS has higher linear than quadratic capability, so its overall lead partly reflects mechanism mix. This does not rescue shared transfer because shared also loses the minimum-mechanism, phase and independent-ablation gates.
- The normalized score is continuous and can be zero-clipped. Small differences near zero are not equivalent to robust behavioral advantages.

## DECISION

`discard` `shared_meta_update` as implemented and return HYP-0014 to `dormant` at confidence `0.08`. Do not replicate, promote or tune learning rates, gates, features, phase lengths or bank capacity. Preserve every candidate, null cell, plan, result and log.

This is a valid negative quick for the preregistered implementation: all integrity and timing gates passed, the causal ablation was present, strong fixed controls were charged, and all registered success criteria failed. It is not a proof that test-time learning or fast weights are impossible.
