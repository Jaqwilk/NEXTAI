# INTERPRETATION

Learned routing produced some specialization relative to a frozen router, most
clearly at K=8,D=64, and top-2 execution roughly halved expert query work. It
did not deliver a reliable quality advantage over executing all experts, and
its small gains over the frozen router were inconsistent for cold and
worst-file behavior. The evidence supports sparse arithmetic as an engineering
property, not useful anonymous regime discovery at the preregistered effect
size.

Formal frontier membership results from trade-offs such as low resident state;
it is not a substitute for the paired causal quality gates. Strong registered
universal-coding controls were both substantially more accurate and far cheaper
at the declared horizon. The dense-control state failure independently prevents
promotion, but the decision to discard follows from completed source-identical
contrasts.
