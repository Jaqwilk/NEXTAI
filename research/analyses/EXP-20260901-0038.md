# INTERPRETATION

Anonymous environment-stable scalar relations were not a transferable
representation in these worlds. The stability filter made almost no reliable
difference relative to ordinary pooling and was consistently worse than a
data-independent coarse partition. It also selected relations that recursively
amplified error in N-CMAPSS, while simple persistence retained the relevant
slow dynamics.

The experiment nevertheless separates implementation failure from mechanism
failure. Every role was finite and stable, semantic fixtures established
channel-permutation equivariance and absence of slot routing, and all outcomes
completed. The failure is the frozen capability-per-full-cost claim, not a
crash or missing-control result. Changing radius, signature coordinates,
cluster cap, ridge, relation count, clip or prototype grid after observing this
result would be tuning a new mechanism and cannot rescue `HYP-0049`.

# CONFIDENCE

Confidence is `0.995` in completion, seed, immutable hashes, raw metrics and
integrity. Confidence is above `0.999` that the preregistered success
conjunction failed: fixed prototypes beat shared in every cell, required causal
gains changed sign by family, and the best classical control beat shared in
each family. Confidence is `0.99` that the invariance selector itself supplied
no meaningful benefit over pooling because the source-identical roles share
the same extraction, local readout and prediction code. Confidence is `0.98`
in discarding this exact mechanism without tuning. Confidence remains below
`0.20` for generalizing the negative to invariant causal learning generally,
because this was one scalar-affine module language and one seed.

# ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

Scalar pairwise affine relations may be too weak to express multivariate
mechanisms; a genuinely compositional module language could transfer even
though this one does not. That is a different representation and cannot be
introduced as a repair after scoring.

The huge common acquisition charge means a smaller state rarely changes the
full workload. A persistent data service could amortize acquisition, but the
declared end-to-end boundary charges it and choosing a new post-result boundary
would invalidate the comparison.

The continuous-event family does contain reusable local relations, but its
strong contextual Chow--Liu result shows that the observed gain is not unique
to the learned invariant partition. Conversely, N-CMAPSS exposes recursive
rollout weakness that a one-seed change cannot plausibly repair without
changing the rule.

# DECISION

`discard`. Mark `HYP-0049` falsified for the exact signature, radius `0.25`,
cap `64`, three-environment stability gate, four-relation local ridge and
recursive clipping rule. Do not replicate, tune or promote it. Preserve the
complete negative result and semantic fixtures as diagnostic history.
