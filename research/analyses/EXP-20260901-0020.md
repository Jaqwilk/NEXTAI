# INTERPRETATION

The experiment validates a narrow implementation fact: exact doubled Born
environments can be reassociated into a tree without changing conditional
predictions, and the declared dependency depth becomes much smaller. It does
not validate the full hypothesis. Fixed 64-byte context on each side makes
span length differ from total contracted length, so the preregistered linear
slope threshold was not reached. More importantly, tree bookkeeping increased
total work and observed latency, while a unigram control matched loss more
cheaply. Parallelizability without matched-quality total-cost efficiency is
not a NEXTAI mechanism worth replicating here.

# CONFIDENCE

High confidence (`0.95`) that this exact rank-2 spectral Born rule fails its
preregistered conjunctive quick criteria. Equality of learned-role quality and
the critical-path counts are deterministic source-identical properties, but
quality estimates use one seed and locally visible files. The three-point
slopes are screening-only, algorithmic operation estimates are not hardware
speedups, and measured latency is load-dependent. These uncertainties cannot
reverse the failed `>0.8` gate or the roughly 31-fold R16-work disadvantage to
unigram.

# ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

The sequential slope failure partly reflects the benchmark's fixed 128 bytes
of surrounding context rather than non-linear sequential contraction work.
Rank two and a single bigram singular component may also be too weak to exploit
nonlocal byte structure. Both are credible, but changing the span axis, rank,
fit or smoothing after this result would change the preregistered exact rule
and is prohibited rather than an authorized rescue.

quality-invariant tree contraction and reduced dependency depth as diagnostic

promising tensor-network principle. This one-seed quick cannot promote.
