A nonparametric dictionary readout does not rescue the frozen width-32 recurrent representation. This strengthens the account that the recurrent support/query state lacks the sufficient relational structure needed by the evaluator, rather than failure being solely a linear-classifier artifact.

The experiment does not falsify all external memory or compositional dictionaries. Its dictionary operates over the failed recurrent state and therefore tests the registered readout alternative, not a graph-structured fragment learner with its own representation. Two decisive failures now make further decoder, width, distance or retrieval tuning on v3 low-information and contrary to the stop-patching rule.
