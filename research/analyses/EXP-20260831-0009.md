# INTERPRETATION

The six-rate table can regularize pooled source learning relative to separately fitted tables, but the decisive foreign-family-only ablation shows that the learned rates do not carry a useful family-independent update geometry. The small aggregate advantage over persistence is neither uniform nor meaningful at the frozen effect size, and it costs more end-to-end computation. The evidence is therefore more consistent with pooled coverage or regularization than with discovery of a portable local update law.

The exact mechanism should not be rescued by changing bins, rate grid, initialization, predictor class, or state size. Such changes would be result-contingent tuning of a direction whose preregistered causal transfer test failed decisively.
