## INTERPRETATION

The cause is a boundary mismatch between JSON serialization and a trusted file
loader. It does not depend on a seed, training length, optimizer, observation
distribution or memory size. There is no evidence to select or reject any
scientific arm. Machine-readable competence gates are false because the required
units are absent; they must not be interpreted as observed poor competence.

The no-scoring repair adds `paired_view_mutable_memory_v2`, which converts and
contains the private path before delegating to the unchanged v1 evaluator. It
extends existing version dispatch without activating v2 or consuming another
registration. Fixed test entropy and a mocked phase boundary reproduce the v1
failure and demonstrate that v2 reaches the pre-fit boundary without generating
research arrays or initializing a model. Invalid path/hash/identity cases are
rejected. Passing these tests establishes repaired plumbing only.

## CONFIDENCE

High confidence in the integration failure and absence of research fit follows
from the traceback, worker phase journal, trusted parent timing and empty trial
journals. Confidence in any learning, competence or economic effect is
unassessable: n=0. The intended five-unit paired 97.5% intervals cannot be
computed and will not be replaced by fixture outcomes or earlier MUC results.
The local visible evaluator is not an externally blinded final.

## ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

Insufficient training and an unsuitable observation task remain possible causes
of a future valid reference failure, but cannot explain this pre-fit exception.
Strong classical transport may match or outperform neural transport; ridge and
kernel controls remain part of the prospective comparison, with their complete
fit and inference costs. Learned alignment followed by exact retrieval is a
separate credible explanation from a neural memory update mechanism.

MUC's public exact key supports a legal symbolic 100% control. Returning to that
task as the sole economic test would not resolve whether a learned observation
alignment has value. MUC stays a technical diagnostic; its completed outcomes,
including the unstable random/hard result, remain unchanged.

## DECISION

**INCONCLUSIVE** for PVM01-REFERENCE-V1; **KEEP** the path-binding diagnosis and
repaired technical boundary. No retry of EXP-20261004-0004, replacement seed,
architecture promotion, final access, WT 8–9, external model/API or schedule
change. Return the terminal v1 cohort to maintenance with scoring=false.
The continuation program remains active; one failed implementation does not
close it. HYP-0012 confidence and the historical BELIEFS draft are unchanged.
