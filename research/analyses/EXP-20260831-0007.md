# INTERPRETATION

The exact unchanged implementation robustly reproduces EXP-0006 under three
runner-random relabelings. The large H96 advantage, finite bounded recurrence,
slot-local reveal updates and K-independent query work are therefore not an
accident of one channel numbering. This is useful evidence for the narrow
mechanism: a compact learned recurrent residual transition plus bounded local
adaptation can outperform frozen persistence, FIR adaptive filters, transition
banks and replay on these physical changepoints after full cost accounting.

It is not yet evidence of cross-source transfer or a novel principle beyond
classical controlled system identification and RLS. The seed design did not
vary the physical trajectories, and the experiment still cannot separate the
causal contribution of recurrence from the contribution of slot-local RLS.
The result supports moving to an adversarial different-source test, not more
WT tuning.

# ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

- Slot-local RLS may provide most of the benefit while the learned recurrent
  representation contributes little. The frozen controls are strong but do
  not include a source-identical recurrence-without-update ablation.
- The ±4 standardized residual bound may be the decisive stability prior.
  Changing it now would be outcome-informed tuning and is forbidden.
- All seeds are nearly identical because exact permutation equivariance makes
  the seed intervention scientifically weak once that property is confirmed.
- Classical DMDc/VAR plus RLS may completely explain the result. Replication
  establishes robustness on this cohort, not architectural novelty.

# DECISION

`keep`, but do not mark `promising` or `promoted`. Preserve EXP-0007 as valid
multi-seed screen evidence of permutation robustness and full-cost Pareto
non-dominance. Stop further tuning or same-source relabeling replications of
this implementation. Ridge `0.001`, features, bound, recurrence and update
law remain frozen.
