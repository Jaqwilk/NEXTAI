# INTERPRETATION

The literal two-channel bottleneck is too restrictive for recursive OOD rollout under the anonymous nonlinear encoder. Joint training reconstruction plus one-step prediction did not choose a representation observably better than the pre-data pair, and its advantage at some D8 cells reversed strongly at D16. The score therefore did not identify coordinates whose learned dynamics remain sufficient under recursive extrapolation.

The shuffled role's lower error shows that the shared quadratic transition/decoder library can sometimes form a useful compact predictor, but it is evidence against the registered selection principle rather than for it. Ambient ridge retained more public state coordinates, extrapolated much better at D16 and was substantially cheaper after all six chart fits were charged. Zero K slope is real but shared by ridge and several controls, so it does not rescue the missing capability-cost advantage.

Changing chart dimension, replacing literal pairs with a learned linear subspace, altering the reconstruction/prediction weight, using rollout loss, changing the shuffle or modifying ridge/update constants after this result would tune the exact direction. Those variants are not authorized. The result falsifies the registered pair-chart selection rule, not representation learning in general.
