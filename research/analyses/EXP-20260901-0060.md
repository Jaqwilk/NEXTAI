# INTERPRETATION

The fixed particle representation preserved bounded, slot-local computation,
but its training residual curves were not a sufficient predictive state for
unseen real WT changepoints. Conditional feature weighting barely improved the
unconditional bootstrap mixture, and the posterior mean's clear advantage over
the median shows that the proposed multimodal aggregation was harmful under the
declared squared-error objective. The failure is not a marginal cost-accounting
technicality: simple persistence was simultaneously better in quality, fit,
query, update, state and all three workload horizons.

This is a valid negative for the exact eight-particle, first-example plus
farthest-first, 31-feature, fixed-temperature, decayed slot-evidence and
weighted-median rule. Changing particle count, features, temperature, evidence
decay, likelihood scale, clustering, aggregation or clipping after this result
would be post-result tuning. The result does not falsify all sequential Monte
Carlo or multimodal state estimation; it falsifies this complete frozen systems
boundary on the visible WT cohort.
