# INTERPRETATION

The exact HYP-0059 mechanism is falsified. Eight-step Jacobi source labels plus
the fixed local ridge map do not extract a cross-application invariant that
improves held-out solves beyond the same implementation fitted only on the
paired source. Pooling eleven or twelve sources mainly adds meta-fit cost. The
small residual changes are inconsistent in sign and at least an order of
magnitude below the frozen minimum on two targets.

This result does not falsify all learned multigrid or all possible transferable
coarse spaces. It does close the preregistered feature map, label construction,
relaxation depth, ridge value, two-vector candidate construction and data-scope
rule. Changing features, steps, loss, source subset, candidate count or PyAMG
settings after this result would be rescue tuning and is forbidden.
