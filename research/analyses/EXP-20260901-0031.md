# INTERPRETATION

This is a valid null and negative result for the exact meta-learned
posterior-partition decision graph. The learned shared utility produced exactly
the same decisions as both the support-only and fixed-weight utilities in all
nine cells. Thus the dramatic reduction in query work comes from compiling a
binary partition policy, not from transferable learning across meta-worlds.
The extra meta data and fitting work bought no measured capability.

The rule also fails the matched-quality requirement: its cost advantage is not
measured at equivalent quality, because Gaussian and kernel correctly answer
15--18 more queries. The low K=`128`, budget=`4` cell is especially important:
the cheap path makes irreversible early splits and cannot recover the quality
of posterior recomputation. This is a concrete weakness of the compiled greedy
partition, not an infrastructure failure.

The experiment does not falsify learned active sensing in general. It
falsifies the preregistered two-feature, nonnegative ridge-learned utility,
depth-16 binary DAG, singleton stopping and nearest-prototype leaf rule on this
cohort. Post-score changes to weights, features, ridge, tree depth, stopping or
leaf inference would be tuning and are prohibited.

# ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

The two learned utility weights may collapse to a ranking equivalent to equal
weights over these Gaussian worlds. That is the tested null, not a reason to
retune: changing features or regularization after observing equality would
define a different mechanism. Thirty-two queries per cell give wide cell-level
intervals, but sampling uncertainty cannot explain the exact shared/support/
frozen equality across every cell or satisfy a nine-answer aggregate margin.

Greedy median thresholds may throw away posterior information that the
adaptive Gaussian and kernel controls retain. A soft or revisable tree could
address that weakness, but it would abandon the tested compiled-decision
principle and likely increase query work. Observe-all's frontier position also
reflects this evaluator's accounting convention; even ignoring it, shared
independently fails both causal-ablation and matched-probe quality gates.

# DECISION

promote or tune it. Preserve the decision-DAG implementation and result as
diagnostic history showing that cheap compiled active paths can reduce work but
that the learned meta-world utility supplied no transferable advantage.
