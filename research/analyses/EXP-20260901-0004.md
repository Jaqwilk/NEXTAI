# INTERPRETATION

Winner-event activation and local count-normalized synapses achieved the
intended sparse-update economics, but direct constant-velocity emission did not
retain robust physical forecasting quality. The failure is concentrated enough
to matter: the worst file misses the preregistered tolerance by `0.1160548160`,
not merely numerical noise. A memory-traffic trade-off alone is insufficient
when a simpler persistence rule is more accurate and cheaper on nearly every
other axis.

This falsifies the exact HYP-0031 circuit and its conjunction, not every form of
local plasticity. Changing the winner threshold, population, emission dynamics,
or learning rule after this result would be post-score family optimization and
is not authorized. Preserve the sparse-cost observation only as a diagnostic
design constraint for genuinely different future mechanisms.

# CONFIDENCE

Confidence is `0.90` that the exact event-sparse constant-velocity circuit
should be discarded. Quality, deterministic operations, memory movement and
three K/horizon scales were measured through the frozen harness with complete
controls and a nonempty frontier. One runner-random seed does not estimate seed
variance and the cohort has only two physical test files; however, the exact
plan required the worst-file gate in this quick, and that gate failed by nearly
twice the meaningful-effect tolerance.

# ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

The anonymous permutation selected by the single seed could alter which tied or
near-tied event wins numerically, although the conformance fixture proves exact
permutation equivariance and the physical data rarely contain exact ties. A
multi-timescale or nonlinear emission might improve the worst file, but that is
a different mechanism and would constitute tuning this family after observing
its error. The benchmark's acquisition cost also dominates total R1 workload,
which makes the update advantage look smaller end to end rather than causing
the quality failure.

# DECISION

promote, or tune it. Retain the valid result and its positive sparse-update
signature as diagnostic history.
