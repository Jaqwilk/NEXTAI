# INTERPRETATION

This is a valid negative for the exact eight-bin meta-learned residual
likelihood. Learning a relabeling-invariant observation model across worlds was
implemented and transferred without leakage, but it changed only one of 288
answers relative to support-only and performed slightly worse than the correctly
specified quadratic likelihood and adaptive kernel. The learned calibration did
not create a useful new representation; on this Gaussian-noise generator it
mostly estimated a discretized approximation to a likelihood already available
to a much cheaper classical control.

The shared/support contrast also rejects the claim that three previous worlds
provide a meaningful observation-economy advantage under this rule. Extra meta
data multiplied acquisition and fit cost without improving minimum-cell
accuracy. This does not reject all learned active perception: it rejects the
preregistered residual bins, smoothing, posterior and variance-greedy probe
policy on this synthetic family.

The plan phrase “strongest complete implementable control” would be impossible
if read literally as requiring a `0.03` accuracy gain over the 100%-accurate
observe-all ceiling. The scientifically matched contrast is to controls using
the same 4/8/16 probe budgets. This wording defect does not create the negative:
shared independently fails both source-identical margins and loses to matched
Gaussian and kernel controls. It does prohibit any positive interpretation and
must not be copied into a successor plan.

# ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

The fixed generator uses homoscedastic Gaussian noise, directly favoring the
quadratic Gaussian control. Learned calibration might matter under multimodal,
heteroscedastic or sensor-dependent corruption, but adding such conditions now
would change the task after observing the result rather than replicate this
rule. Eight residual bins may also be too coarse, yet changing bins or
smoothing is explicitly forbidden post-score tuning.

Only 32 queries populate each cell, so one-answer cell differences have wide
uncertainty. That uncertainty makes the small apparent shared/support advantage
inconclusive rather than positive; it cannot bridge the `0.03` registered
margin. The all-pairs calibration implementation also has high fit peak and
operation cost; a streaming implementation could reduce memory but cannot
retroactively supply the missing quality signal.

# DECISION

Discard and falsify `HYP-0042` for this exact mechanism. Do not tune the bin
edges, smoothing, variance floor, posterior, tie rule, stopping or probe score;
do not replicate and do not promote. Preserve active information acquisition as
a useful classical component, while rejecting this meta-learned likelihood as
a distinct capability-per-full-cost principle.
