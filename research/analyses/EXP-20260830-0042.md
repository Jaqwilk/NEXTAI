## INTERPRETATION

The causal intervention worked mechanically but did not rescue the research claim. Equality-canonicalized pointer decoding removed the clearest EXP-0041 implementation defect and produced a large absolute improvement over raw-ID regression, especially on probabilistic and predictive cases. Nevertheless, it failed completely on local dynamics, nearly completely on program composition, missed the `0.90` per-family gate by a large margin, and did not establish a meaningful shared-training advantage over the source-identical independent ablation.

The result separates two explanations. Raw numeric regression was indeed a major implementation error, because a faithful pointer head increased transfer from `0.015625` to `0.177083`. But output symmetry was not the main remaining obstacle: the simpler shared empirical control matched or slightly exceeded it at lower full cost, while native specialists solved every family. The pooled SVD plus nearest pointer-template representation did not learn reusable computation across families.

Under the preregistered kill rule, HYP-0015 should become dormant without rank, feature, data-volume or decoder patches. This is strong adverse evidence against this tested cross-family route, not a theorem that no future shared learner can exist.

## ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

- Nearest-template decoding may be too weak to express multi-step local dynamics and program composition even when pointer symmetry is correct. That limits how broadly the negative result generalizes, but it was explicitly preregistered as the minimal faithful implementation and cannot be patched after seeing the score.
- Syntax-only canonicalization treats every integer payload as a possible atom. Numeric quantities and opaque identifiers can therefore share one address space; separating them would require a richer learned type mechanism or a forbidden hand-written ontology.
- Fixed-width serialization can discard native sufficient statistics. The exact specialist controls show the generated worlds are solvable, but they receive native public objects and therefore do not isolate serialization loss.
- Four pooled meta examples per family and one training seed may be insufficient. Increasing data now would be post-result tuning, and the simpler empirical shared control already dominates the pointer learner at the observed budget.
- The apparent pointer gain over EXP-0041 combines equality canonicalization with pointer-template decoding as one registered output-representation factor. It does not identify which subcomponent supplies the gain.

## DECISION

`discard` `shared_cross_family_learner` as implemented and make HYP-0015 `dormant` at confidence `0.08`. Do not replicate, promote or patch this route. Preserve the candidate, failed cells, plan, result, logs and analysis as scientific history.

The valid quick satisfies every preregistered kill condition: overall transfer is `0.177083`, minimum family is `0`, the two-query shared advantage over independent training is negligible and cost-disfavored, the simpler shared empirical model dominates it on observed quality and cost, and the specialist contextual suite is the sole implementable Pareto point.
