# OBSERVATION

The audited quick realized runner-random seed `53162585`. Thirteen of fourteen
roles completed all 18 trials; `wt_train_only_discretized_cssr_v1` timed out and
remains a recorded mandatory-control failure. Evaluator integrity passed before
and after scoring. The main error-gated trace was finite and stable in every
cell and entered the implementable Pareto frontier.

The main aggregate NRMSE was `1.002571393596196`, versus
`1.0071290063754978` for both frozen credit and the exact-control-level bank,
`1.0043389572256034` for shuffled temporal credit, and
`1.001643348926254` for dense credit. Thus its aggregate gain over the strongest
relevant frozen/control baseline was only `0.0045576127793018895`, far below
the frozen meaningful effect `0.1325268421060828`. Its H32 and H96 gains over
frozen credit were respectively `0.005408036875704125` and
`0.0041934222246178265`, also far below the gate. Dense credit was more accurate
than the main at aggregate, H16, H32 and H96; the main beat dense in only the
K=54/H=96 cell, by `0.0000430456300804` NRMSE.

Error gating executed about `65.64%` of dense credit's incremental update work.
It reduced aggregate update operations by `13.56%` and touched bytes by `18.98%`
relative to dense, with strictly lower values in every K/horizon cell. The
aligned main also beat shuffled credit in all nine cells, but the aggregate
effect was only `0.0017675636294074426`. Stable rollout was `1.0`; main worst-file
and worst-transition NRMSE were `1.1288436682442058` and
`1.5661700779096945`.

# INTERPRETATION

The exact rule is falsified. Sparse gating provides a real accounting tradeoff,
and aligned weights are marginally better than their frozen permutation, but
neither effect approaches the preregistered quality threshold. More importantly,
dense use of the same credit rule is slightly more accurate on the declared
aggregate and all three horizon summaries. The simple control-level bank obtains
almost the same quality with zero adaptation work and much smaller state. Pareto
membership therefore reflects a tiny quality/cost tradeoff, not the required
fundamental capability-per-cost improvement.

The CSSR timeout prevents promotion or a claim against every mandatory control,
but it does not rescue the hypothesis: all causal ablations completed, and the
main decisively failed its frozen margin against frozen credit, shuffled credit,
dense credit and the complete exact-control bank. No post-result threshold,
decay, feature, learning-rate or clipping adjustment is permitted.

# CONFIDENCE

Confidence in HYP-0052 decreases from `0.08` to `0.01`, status `falsified`.
This is one valid screening seed, so it is evidence against this exact rule,
not proof that all biologically inspired eligibility mechanisms fail. Uncertainty
remains about faithful CSSR performance because its process timed out, and about
mechanisms with learned rather than fixed temporal features; neither uncertainty
licenses tuning this implementation.

# ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

- Nine post-artifact reveals per held-out file may be too few to amortize local
  credit learning, but that limitation was preregistered and is part of this
  exact mechanism's full-system test.
- Most capability comes from the frozen residual bank: frozen credit exactly
  matches the control-level bank, while online roles add only milliscale gains.
- The aligned-versus-shuffled gap may reflect generic recency weighting rather
  than discovered temporal credit; both use the same fixed feature family.
- CSSR might offer a better quality/cost point if allowed to finish, but the
  main already fails causal and complete-control criteria independently of it.

# DECISION

Discard and do not replicate. End the exact feature map, trace decay `0.75`,
error gate `0.90`, eta `0.05`, clipping and residual-bank rule without tuning.
Preserve the complete result, timeout, logs, plan and code as negative history.

# NEXT DISCRIMINATING EXPERIMENT

Do not create WT v5 or another eligibility variant. The next scored breadth
experiment should test a fundamentally different observation-learned mechanism:
one shared active experiment compiler that learns a small set of anonymous
intervention operators from earlier public trajectories, composes them for an
unseen intervention sequence, and locally installs only a verified operator
without global refit. Use an existing intervention/recombination cohort if its
public interface can hide native operator identities and separate train from OOD
compositions; otherwise spend at most one service wake freezing the smallest
identifiable role-only successor before candidate implementation. Compare an
unchanged shared learner with independent, shuffled-assignment and frozen-library
ablations plus exact local-causal, empirical, autoregressive and privileged
support controls. A one-seed quick must require OOD composition quality, local
update, three-scale full cost and universal Pareto non-dominance and cannot
promote.
