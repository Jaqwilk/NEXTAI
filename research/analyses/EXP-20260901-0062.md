# INTERPRETATION

Input-dependent retention learned a small advantage over fixed retention, but
the advantage was below the preregistered noise-derived threshold and did not
beat a memoryless version of the same learner. At larger training scales the
retained recurrent state was actively harmful. The likely mechanism-level
failure is therefore not merely an inefficient implementation: under this
frozen one-epoch width-16 rule, the learned byte emission map carries useful
signal while temporal recurrence fails to preserve predictive context.

PPM-D and CTW show that this real repository cohort contains exploitable
sequential structure. The negative cannot be explained by an intrinsically
unpredictable dataset. It closes the exact diagonal update, shared-row
initialization, width, optimizer schedule and chunking rule without tuning.
It does not falsify all selective state-space models, Mamba, S4 or recurrence
in general.

# CONFIDENCE

Confidence is `0.995` in seed provenance, measurements and integrity. Confidence
is above `0.999` that the preregistered success conjunction failed because both
the causal-ablation margin and classical-control requirements failed. Confidence
is `0.98` in discarding this exact rule without replication or post-result
tuning. Confidence is below `0.20` for extending the negative to larger or
fundamentally different state-space architectures. The dense-control crash
prevents promotion but cannot rescue the completed negative contrasts.

# ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

One training epoch and width 16 may underfit, while truncated chunks may hinder
long credit assignment. Testing different width, epochs, learning rate, chunk
length, initialization or optimizer after seeing this result would, however,
be tuning of the exact mechanism and is forbidden by the G1 window. The
resident-state advantage also explains why the main remains Pareto-listed, but
does not reverse its large quality and workload deficits. Finally, the first
fit's allocator peak may include framework initialization; it is reported as a
systems observation rather than used as the causal falsifier.
