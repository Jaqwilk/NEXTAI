# INTERPRETATION

This is a valid negative for the exact 64-state surprise-gated transition rule,
not an implementation failure. Event selection genuinely removed about 45% of
reveal-update work and improved accuracy relative to a dense clock, but the
learned transitions moved the system away from the much stronger frozen
unigram-like state partition. The equality-pattern state and bounded majority
transition vote did not capture the variable-length repository structure that
PPM-D and CTW exploit. Preserving state through predictable stretches is thus a
real scheduling property here, not a useful learned representation.

The dense autoregressive crash prevents any positive promotion claim but is not
needed for the negative: both causal ablations, PPM-D and CTW completed every
trial, and frozen-transition alone dominates main. Changing the threshold,
state width, smoothing, signature or transition vote after seeing this seed
would be a new mechanism rather than replication and is disallowed.

# CONFIDENCE

Confidence is high (`0.98`) that this exact preregistered conjunction fails.
All 45 main/frozen loss contrasts have the wrong sign, the aggregate gaps are
over twice the meaningful-effect threshold, and main is Pareto-dominated.
One locally visible corpus and one runner seed do not falsify all event-driven
learning, but they cannot justify tuning or replication of this rule.

# ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

The 6-bit threshold may be too conservative, equality-pattern states may omit
content needed for code, and bounded Boyer-Moore votes may forget multimodal
transitions. A probabilistic transition tensor or learned state merge might
behave differently. These are plausible new algorithms, but every one changes
a frozen causal commitment after observing scoring output. The completed dense
and frozen ablations already show that neither more events nor learned
transitions as implemented supplies the missing quality.

# DECISION

Discard and falsify `HYP-0041` for this exact mechanism. Preserve the verified
event-sparse update signature as diagnostic evidence, but do not tune the
threshold, state width, state signature, smoothing or transition vote; do not
replicate or promote. The result remains `complete_with_failures` because the
dense autoregressive control exceeded state, while its scientific negative
contrasts remain valid.
