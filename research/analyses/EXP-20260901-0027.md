# INTERPRETATION

This is a valid negative for the exact sparse learned energy-factor rule, not
an implementation crash. Fixed skip-lag factors do learn strong associations,
but synchronous self-conditioning amplifies wrong high-confidence assignments
inside long masked spans. The one-sweep causal ablation being better shows that
additional relaxation is harmful rather than merely unnecessary. The frozen
role's exact equality with unigram isolates the damage to learned factors, and
PPM's nonzero exact spans show the benchmark contains predictable structure
that this energy parameterization failed to use.

The bounded critical path and monotone registered energy are real qualitative
properties, but energy monotonicity is not aligned with conditional likelihood:
the dynamics can descend its learned objective while moving away from the true
masked bytes. After earlier energy experiments reduced to classical decoding
and lost full cost, this real-byte failure supports abandoning the current
energy-landscape family rather than tuning lags, smoothing, clipping, sweeps or
acceptance.

# CONFIDENCE

High confidence (`0.98`) that this exact preregistered rule fails every causal
and matched-quality success criterion. Loss gaps of `1.25` to one-sweep and
`7.85` to frozen factors are far larger than the frozen `0.05` margin and have
the wrong sign; exact-span gain is zero versus both ablations and negative
versus PPM. The result uses one seed and locally visible files, and the
three-span slopes are screening-only. Those limitations prevent a universal
claim about all energy-based models but cannot reverse this rule's conjunctive
failure or justify post-score tuning.

# ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

Hard argmax messages may be more brittle than soft mean-field messages, and
lags `1/2/4/8` may be mismatched to repository byte structure. Add-half
smoothing or ±4 clipping may also overstate rare skip associations. All are
plausible alternative implementations, but changing any of them after seeing
this seed would violate the immutable plan. Moreover, the core iteration
intervention itself worsened its source-identical one-sweep control, so a new
variant would not be a replication of the tested principle.

# DECISION

Discard the exact sparse learned positional energy-factor mechanism and mark
`HYP-0007` falsified at low confidence for portfolio purposes. Do not replicate
or tune this rule. Preserve the passing monotone-energy, equivariance and
bounded-depth fixtures as diagnostic controls, and preserve the one-sweep row
on the frontier without interpreting it as evidence for iterative energy
reasoning. This one-seed quick cannot promote.
