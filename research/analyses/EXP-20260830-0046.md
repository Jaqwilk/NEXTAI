# INTERPRETATION

Removing family-dependent truncation did not rescue shared representation learning. The pooled local-relation space captured some probabilistic and predictive regularities, but it learned nothing usable for unseen local-dynamics worlds and almost nothing for program composition. This is a direct failure of the intended cross-family signature, not a marginal miss.

The independent ablation's two-query advantage and lower R16 work show no measurable benefit from pooling representations across families. The eighteen tied cells indicate that both variants often reduce to the same coarse nearest-template behavior. Lower shared state does not rescue the claim because both capability gates fail by large margins.

The specialist result establishes that the generated test worlds are solvable under the registered budgets. It does not prove that arbitrary shared learners cannot transfer; it shows that this lossless hashed local-relation/PCA mechanism does not recover the family-native sufficient computations. Increasing rank, changing features, adding typed heads or tuning family clusters after this result would change or patch the registered thesis.

# ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

- Syntax-only lossless serialization preserves values but may not expose the relations needed for native tasks in a learnable geometry. That is a limitation of this common interface/mechanism, not evidence that shared representation is impossible.
- Three fixed training seeds may be insufficient to learn cross-world invariants. Increasing them now would be post-result data tuning, and the independent ablation already shows no positive sharing signal at the registered amount.
- Structural shape can reveal family statistically. The candidate used no explicit label or branch, but its learned similarity may still implicitly cluster families. This would favor, not explain away, the failed transfer.
- The shared contextual control is a generic tree-selected relational projection rather than the native exact contextual Chow–Liu computation. The negative conclusion does not depend on that approximation because the source-identical sharing ablation fails and the native contextual suite solves all families.
- Operation counts are implementation estimates. Their calibration cannot reverse zero local accuracy, near-zero program accuracy or the missing sharing advantage.
