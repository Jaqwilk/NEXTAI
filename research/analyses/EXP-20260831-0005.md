# INTERPRETATION

The exact five-bit predictive-equivalence index does not learn a representation that transfers
useful local operators across these unseen heterogeneous worlds. Its learned global key sometimes
helps relative to independent fitting in DronePropA and continuous-event, but that effect does not
generalize to DS08a. More decisively, a code learned only from other families is uniformly worse
than constructing the same index from target support, so the required transferable representation
is absent.

The zero K slope establishes only bounded lookup after an expensive learned index exists. It is
not a favorable scaling signature at matched quality: persistence is substantially more accurate,
stable and cheaper end to end. Changing sign-bit count, target moments, representation ridge,
bucket cap, local ridge or thresholds after this result would be outcome-informed tuning and is
forbidden by the plan.

# CONFIDENCE

Confidence in HYP-0027 falls from `0.10` to `0.01`. Confidence is above `0.99` that the exact
preregistered conjunction is false on the realized seed. Cross-seed generality remains formally
uncertain, but reversing all nine negative cross-family contrasts, the three DS08a shared
contrasts, the stability failure and the large persistence gap is not plausible enough to justify
replication under the frozen quick policy.

# ALTERNATIVE EXPLANATIONS

- A different runner seed could change magnitudes, but the failure is multi-family, multi-K and
  far larger than the frozen development noise floor in the mechanical family.
- Five permutation-invariant target moments may be too lossy. Replacing them or changing the bit
  count is a new mechanism, not a repair or replication of HYP-0027.
- The local affine operator may be insufficient after routing. Changing it would confound the
  learned-key question and violates the frozen matched-control design.
- The `0.95` capability gate makes the frontier empty for the cohort, but it does not create the
  observed negative causal contrasts or the direct quality/cost loss to persistence.

# DECISION

`discard` the exact predictive-equivalence index and mark HYP-0027 `falsified`. Do not tune or
replicate it. Preserve EXP-0005 as valid negative evidence and EXP-0004 separately as invalid
diagnostic history. The result rejects this implementation-level principle; it does not falsify
all learned representations, indexing, predictive states or local-update architectures and does
not support an LLM-successor or general scaling claim.
