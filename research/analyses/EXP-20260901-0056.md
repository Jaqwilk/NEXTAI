# INTERPRETATION

Node-conditional routing repaired the catastrophic flat-key quality loss of
EXP-0054 relative to dense access and produced a real R1 amortization signal.
It did not show that learning created the useful address. The untrained frozen
encoder was exact, cheaper and Pareto-relevant, while learned training reduced
large-K accuracy and contributed most of the full workload. The exact raw k-d
tree then supplied the same local-access behavior with higher quality and far
lower fit, state and repeated-use cost.

The result therefore separates two facts. Balanced local indexing works in
this world, but the registered observation-learned pair representation does
not improve it. The large-K R4096 slope also shows that the charged fit and
access path remain too coupled to knowledge size for the requested scaling
signature. Changing the pair loss, encoder, split objective, beam, visit cap or
verification after this result would tune the rejected exact mechanism and is
not authorized.

This is narrow support for HYP-0012's full-system accounting thesis, not a
general impossibility result. The benchmark is locally visible and synthetic,
and only one runner seed was executed.

recorded rather than tested as a descendant.

The raw k-d tree benefits from the synthetic raw geometry. That limits external

explanation for this result.
