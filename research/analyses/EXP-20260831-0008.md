# INTERPRETATION

The WT result does not transfer through this exact family-blind affine recurrent
residual rule. Pooling helps strongly on DronePropA and moderately on the
continuous-event family, but causes severe negative transfer on N-CMAPSS. More
importantly, training only on the other families is worse than using public
support alone in every cell. The learned slow state therefore does not encode a
portable representation shared by the three worlds.

The failure is fundamental for the preregistered rule, not an implementation
crash: all four causal roles completed, remained finite, used identical source
and constants, passed permutation/session-local fixtures and were evaluated at
all K values. Changing ridge, correction bound, features, normalization or
channel alignment now would be outcome-informed tuning and is forbidden.
