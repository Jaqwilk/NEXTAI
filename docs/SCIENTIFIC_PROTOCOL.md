## 1. Three questions, three evidential standards

A mechanism test asks whether learned information causes a specified effect.
It requires a source-identical intervention, a frozen/no-learning or shuffled
control, meaningful classical alternatives, a functioning positive control,
a preregistered effect size and uncertainty. It may yield a narrow positive
without a full-cost win or unrelated-family transfer.

An economics test asks whether useful matched quality costs less. It requires
the best relevant implementable controls, the full boundary, hardware and
deployment scenarios, quality guards and a replicated non-dominated result.
Lower quality at lower cost is not matched-quality superiority. Better quality
at greater cost can be a trade-off, not a cost victory.

A transfer test asks whether the learned effect generalizes as declared.
It additionally requires independent held-out units, source-identical learned
code/state/update rules, no privileged target information, and controls that
isolate transferred information. Unrelated-family transfer is required only
for a cross-family claim, not for every local learning experiment.

Do not translate any of these into a claim of general intelligence, an LLM
successor or a new scaling law from a toy task.

## 2. Positive and negative measurement controls first

Demonstrate competent learning on a task/recipe where the control is appropriate.
Freeze a feasible training budget and training/development criterion from primary
prior art; retain loss curves, optimizer steps, parameter count and device.
Do not infer failure of learning from an undertrained model or broken evaluator.
A conventional transformer is not required to learn a local update rule or
beat PPM/CTW on arbitrary tiny data to certify a learning measurement.

Check learning sensitivity, quality calculation, leakage, device synchronization
and timing independently. Intentionally broken controls must fail the intended
test without changing the evaluator to favor any candidate. Diagnostic results
are separately identified and cannot enter architecture frontiers.

## 3. Preregistration and bounded development

Before implementing a tested change, freeze its question, data/split, recipe,
controls, causal factors, effect sizes, full metrics, budget and outcome policy.
A screening study changes one fundamental factor; a factorial interaction study
may change several only when all contrasts are preregistered.

Development is allowed on declared train/dev data within a finite attempt and
compute cap. Every failed, crashed or discarded attempt remains recorded.
Select and freeze one recipe before final test access. Final test data never
guide tuning, early stopping, threshold choice or baseline selection.
After a negative final result, close the tested version. A new claim requires a
new plan, fresh holdout, disclosed earlier results and an explicit reason.

Local visible data remain screening/development evidence even if code enforces
predict-before-reveal. Strong claims require an independently isolated evaluator
or holdout the research agent cannot inspect. Never claim blindness from a name.

## 4. Fair baselines and full cost

Include a simplest heuristic, a competent learned control where relevant,
matched causal ablations and the strongest relevant classical algorithm.
A classical solver exploiting legal structure is implementable; only actual
privileged information makes an oracle. Do not require a learned algorithm to
be mathematically outside all polynomial-time statistics: that is not a valid
empirical criterion for learning or practical advantage.

Count observation acquisition, encoding, preprocessing, retrieval/index builds,
training/fit, queries, output decoding, state/cache maintenance and updates.
Separate training/R&D from inference; give an explicitly amortized workload as
well. Specify what R repeats: a query, stream or full workload. Derive break-even
reuse without disguising the workload unit. Report memory and affected bytes.

Keep operation counts/estimated FLOPs separate from measured latency, throughput
and energy. Compare same-hardware implementations as well as explicitly labeled
best-feasible deployment options. Record hardware, software, precision, threads,
synchronization, warm-up, batches, load, p50/p95, raw repeats, RAM and VRAM.
Measure batch=1 end-to-end latency separately from batched throughput. Amortized
batch time is not single-query latency. Never infer energy if it was not measured.

## 5. Replication, scale and uncertainty

At least three seeds are needed for a replicated positive, with all outcomes
retained. Seeds, permutations and repeated samples of the same physical trace
are not independent data units. Report both sources of variation explicitly.
Resample/aggregate at the independently held-out unit, not the overlapping window.

Scaling needs at least three distinct scale points for an inferential slope,
a useful range, uncertainty, fixed quality and independent variation of K and D.
K denotes stored knowledge in the target experiment, not whatever native length
is convenient. If size and task family are confounded, label the slope descriptive.
Adversarial tests attack the claimed signature, not silently change the question.
